## What matters for the time limit

- Everything here is instant except standard SEPA (up to the next business day) and the bank-account side of Tikkie (next working day at the latest).
- HalCash is fast to send, but the seller's risk lasts until they physically withdraw the cash. That is a release rule, not a time-to-pay rule.
- SEPA, SEPA Instant and Wero carry a 13-month fraud recall. The speed is not the problem; the risk afterwards is.
