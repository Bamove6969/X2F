## What matters for the time limit

- Zelle, Interac, Cash App, Venmo, Apple Cash, Chime and Strike all arrive in minutes in the normal case. The time limit is really about how long a person needs to open the app and send. Allow a margin for the occasional first-time or review hold.
- ACH and wires only move on business days. A fixed clock is unfair to a buyer who takes a trade on Friday night, unless the clock covers the weekend or skips it.
- ACH can still be reversed for days after it lands. That affects when the seller should release, not how long the buyer has to pay.
