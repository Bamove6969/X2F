## What matters for the time limit

- Wallet-to-wallet sends (PayPal, Skrill, Neteller, Volet, Paxum, Uphold) are instant.
- Remittance services are fast **for cash pickup and Express**, but slow if the sender funds them from a bank account or picks the economy option. The ad terms should say which option the buyer must use.
- Cash pickup also needs the **seller** to walk to an agent before releasing, because the sender can cancel until the cash is collected.
- Swift wires are the slowest bank method in the catalog after ACH and cash by mail.
