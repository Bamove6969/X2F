Checked 9 Nov 2026. Group 8 of 8. Methods: AMAZON_GIFT_CARD, UBER_GIFT_CARD, VISA_GIFT_CARD, MASTERCARD_GIFT_CARD, APPLE_GIFT_CARD, GOOGLE_PLAY_GIFT_CARD, STEAM_GIFT_CARD, RAZER_GOLD_GIFT_CARD, XBOX_GIFT_CARD, PLAYSTATION_GIFT_CARD, WALMART_GIFT_CARD, TARGET_GIFT_CARD, SEPHORA_GIFT_CARD, OTHER_GIFT_CARD, PAYSAFE, F2F, CASH_DEPOSIT, CASH_AT_ATM, PAY_BY_MAIL, US_POSTAL_MONEY_ORDER (20).

**How to read this.** For gift cards, "receiving" happens the moment the buyer sends the code in the trade chat and the seller redeems or checks it. So the real delay is **how long the buyer needs to get a card**: minutes if they already hold one, up to an hour for an eGift bought online (Amazon's own figure), or a trip to a shop. For cash, the delay is the person's own travel time or the post. "Earlier file" means `research/payment-methods/gift-cards.md` or `global.md` (21 Oct).

## What matters for the time limit

- For gift cards and PaysafeCard, the code is "received" the moment it is posted in chat. The time limit should cover **buying the card**, which for most people is a shop trip or an online purchase: about an hour, rarely more.
- For cash in person, the timer should follow the agreed meeting, not a fixed number.
- For cash by mail and money orders, the post takes 1–5 business days. That is far longer than the builder's current 24-hour maximum for time to pay (see REPORT.md).
