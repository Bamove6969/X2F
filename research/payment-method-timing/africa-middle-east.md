## What matters for the time limit

- Mobile money is instant. The risk is the reversal process: M-PESA lets the sender ask within 24 hours, and MTN MoMo allows weeks, as long as the money has not been spent or withdrawn. That is a release rule for the seller, not a time-to-pay rule.
- Most timing here is assumed from how the systems work, not from official pages. Treat the confidence as low.
