## What matters for the time limit

- Almost everything here is instant. The exceptions are Japan (some banks still credit the next business day) and India's bank-account rails (NEFT in batches; NEFT, RTGS and IMPS often need a new beneficiary added first, which can have a cooling period).
- UPI needs no beneficiary set-up, so it is the fastest Indian option for a buyer.
