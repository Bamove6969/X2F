## What matters for the time limit

- All of these are instant in the normal case.
- UK Faster Payments now has a legal 4-business-day hold when the sending bank suspects fraud. That is rare, but if it happens the buyer will miss any short timer through no fault of their own. The dispute process needs to cover this.
- The Russian methods have similar 2-day anti-fraud holds.
