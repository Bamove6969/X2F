Competitor research · 14 Oct 2026

After LocalMonero

Who used to let people sell Monero for cash or bank transfer with escrow, why they died, what is left, and where a new app can win.

The short version

  • Every company that held users' coins in the middle is gone. Regulation, compliance cost or the operators themselves ended each one.
  • The survivors that hold no coins (Haveno and its RetoSwap network, Bisq) keep working, but they are desktop-only, clunky and short on traders. RetoSwap also lost about $2.7M to protocol exploits in 2026.
  • The slick, phone-first experience exists only for Bitcoin (Peach, Vexl, RoboSats). Nobody offers it for Monero. That gap is the opening.
  • Escrow is the hard part. Monero has no smart contracts, so escrow means either holding the coins yourself (the LocalMonero model that died) or 2-of-3 multisig (Haveno's model). Monero's upcoming FCMP++ upgrade has no confirmed multisig support yet.
The graveyard

Platforms that closed

LocalMonero / AgoraDesk
2017 – May 2024
Closed
The direct model to replace. Announced 7 May 2024 with only "internal and external factors" as the reason. Trading ended 14 May and the site went offline 7 Nov 2024. The shutdown came weeks after the Samourai Wallet founders were arrested. Sellers paid a 1% fee and posted an arbitration bond. Old forum complaints describe disputes that admins never answered. Announcement, Protos

Some 2026 vendor blogs say AgoraDesk still runs. That contradicts the official shutdown, so I treat it as wrong.

LocalBitcoins
2012 – Feb 2023
Closed
Blamed the "crypto winter". It had been under Finnish regulation since 2019, and in June 2025 regulators fined it €500,000 for weak identity checks. Once you are regulated, compliance is a permanent cost. The Block, Wikipedia
Paxful
2015 – Nov 2025
Closed
Wound down on 1 Nov 2025, blaming misconduct by its former co-founders and unsustainable compliance costs. The Block, Finance Magnates
LocalCryptos
– Oct 2022
Closed
Ceased operations in October 2022; I found no stated reason. CB Insights
Still standing

What people use now

Haveno / RetoSwap
Monero-native
Active
The closest match to LocalMonero: non-custodial 2-of-3 multisig escrow over Tor, with no accounts. Weak spots: desktop-only, low liquidity, both sides must post a security deposit (a first-time buyer has to own crypto before they can buy), and arbitration takes 24–48 hours. An attacker who could impersonate the arbitrator drained about $2.7M on 20 May 2026, and a second exploit halted trading on 17 Jun 2026. Versions 1.9 and 1.10 (Oct 2026) add security hardening and basic features like trade history and notifications. A 2025 academic study also found that Haveno trades can sometimes be traced on-chain. A mobile app is Haveno's most-requested feature. Two community projects are attempting one: an unofficial Android app, and a cross-platform app funded through Monero's crowdfunding system. On iOS, users have to run a remote Haveno node. Android app, CCS proposal Halborn, MEXC, 1.10 release, arXiv, kycnot.me reviews
LocalCoinSwap
multi-coin
Active
The closest working copy of the LocalMonero model, run as a company. Users keep their own keys: a trade locks the seller's coins in an on-chain escrow, and one reviewer had to deposit a little BNB to accept escrow, which points to smart contracts. Monero support is unconfirmed; sources contradict each other, and one lists it as not accepted. 2026 reviews report surprise identity checks as trading volume grows, funds held until ID was provided, blocked accounts, and some disputes decided in a scammer's favour. The UK Financial Conduct Authority lists it as possibly providing financial services without permission, even though it never holds users' coins. kycnot.me, NoKYC Index, Trustpilot, GNcrypto
Bisq
BTC-first
Active
Slow trades and a clunky app; Monero isn't native. Bisq 2 is a rewrite in progress. kycnot.me
Peach, Vexl, RoboSats, Hodl Hodl, Mostro
Bitcoin only
Not Monero
Worth copying for UX ideas, not competing with. Peach is a beginner-friendly mobile app where the buyer posts no deposit. RoboSats uses small Lightning bonds and no accounts. Vexl matches trades through your phone contacts. Mostro runs over Nostr. Spark comparison, BitcoinRamps
Instant swap services
crypto ↔ crypto
Not P2P
ChangeNOW, FixedFloat and similar services swap one coin for another. They don't pay out fiat and often enforce identity checks above limits. Overview
Pain points

What hurts when you sell XMR for fiat

The seller carries most of the risk in an off-ramp trade, because the fiat side can be reversed or turn out to be dirty money.

  1. Chargebacks. The buyer pays with a reversible method, receives the XMR, then disputes the payment with their bank.
  2. Frozen bank accounts. A scammer pays with money stolen from a fraud victim. When the victim reports it, the seller's account gets frozen. Third-party payers are the red flag. TechCabal, Binance guidance
  3. Fake payment confirmations. Screenshots or SMS messages that look like bank alerts when no money arrived.
  4. No one to trade with. Few buyers, and fewer still who use your payment method in your country.
  5. Getting started is hard. Desktop installs, Tor setup, and deposits required before your first trade.
  6. Slow or silent disputes. Arbitration takes 24–48 hours on Haveno, and some LocalMonero disputes reportedly got no answer at all.
  7. Surprise identity checks. Counterparties on "no-KYC" platforms ask for ID on larger trades.
  8. Protocol security. Two RetoSwap exploits in 2026 drained escrowed funds.

Escrow

How escrow can work for Monero

ModelHow it worksUser experienceRisk
We hold the coins
LocalMonero		The seller deposits XMR with us; we release it to the buyer.The simplest flow.The operator is a money transmitter needing licences and identity checks. This is the model that died.
2-of-3 multisig
Haveno		Buyer, seller and arbitrator each hold one key, and any two can release the coins. We never hold funds alone.Complex today, but good design can hide that.Large attack surface (see RetoSwap). Multisig is unavailable on the FCMP++ test network, and the upgrade has no mainnet date. TokenPost
Bonds and reputation only
Vexl-style		No escrow; parties post bonds or rely on trust.The lightest flow.Weak protection for strangers.

Legal caution: in the US, a jury convicted Tornado Cash developer Roman Storm in 2025 of conspiring to run an unlicensed money-transmitting business, even though his code never held users' funds. His retrial is set for April 2027. Not holding custody lowers the risk; it doesn't remove it. Talk to a lawyer before launch. JD Supra, The Block

The opening

Where a new app can win

Peach-quality UX for Monero

A phone-first web app with a guided sell flow, plain language and no setup. Nobody offers this for XMR.

Seller protection built in

Rank payment methods by how easily they can be reversed, and prompt the seller to check that the payer's name matches. Show "check your bank app" before Release unlocks. Gather evidence for disputes automatically.

Borrow liquidity

Build a better client on top of the existing Haveno network instead of starting with an empty marketplace.

Fast, visible disputes

A published dispute deadline, a live status for each case, and arbitrators who post bonds of their own.

Cheaper first trade

Small or no buyer deposit for low-value trades, as Peach does, to fix the problem of needing crypto before you can buy any.

Security as a feature

Independent audits and a public bug bounty before launch, given RetoSwap's record.
