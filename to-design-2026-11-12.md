## Needs a board from you (he asked for these directly)

1. **First screen (landing).** "Welcome to" runs diagonally from the top left down to a large X2F logo in the middle. Two choices, "Take the tour" and "Skip and get started". The bottom tab bar shows as if they'd already dived in. Links to FAQ, Getting started, Support and Settings fill the empty space. This replaces /sell as the first thing a new person sees.
2. **Guided site tour.** It goes page by page and highlights each important feature in turn, with a quality arrow pointing at the highlighted area and a well-written explanation. "Next" moves to the next feature; after the last feature on a page it becomes "Next screen". He wants this to be high quality. Please draw the tour shell (highlight, arrow, card, controls) and list the stops per page.
3. **Signup welcome (/setup/welcome).** A checklist, every item with one big green tick:
   - (1) "Configured your secure methods of logging into X2F": the chosen methods, best to worst, with ONE tick for the group;
   - (2) "Emergency login method: recovery code documented";
   - (3) "Connected your wallet": the connection method chosen.
   Below the checklist, in bigger font: "Any information provided above can be changed later in settings. Next, you will log in for the first time and test out the security methods you've set up, which will allow us to welcome you to the X2F experience." Plus "Remember, you only pay a fee if you post an ad. Clicking on an ad and starting a trade is always free." He says the screen "needs better aesthetics".
4. **Logo top-left with the screen name beside it, on every screen.** He reminded us this was already his rule.
5. **Spacing and type.** Fonts "should be bigger, too small for our users, but not much bigger, noticeably". Screens like /login and /session-length should move content down so they don't look empty, and use the space for helpful links. /buy is "a little cramped": a little more space between things. He likes the payment-method dropdown ("Good job").
6. **Wallet step (/setup/wallet).** The wallet names shouldn't look like buttons when they do nothing. Offer a real connect method for wallets that have one, plus a plain "suggested wallets" list. This waits on the research; please draw both states.
