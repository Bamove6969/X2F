## Verified (no finding)

- **Tier maths (`packages/trade/src/flexOffer.ts`).**
  - All amounts are bigint, with no float anywhere. FIAT is `^[1-9]\d{0,13}$`, so there is no zero or leading-zero tier edge.
  - Both ends are inclusive. `tiersValid` sorts by bigint and refuses `next.min <= prev.max`, so touching ranges count as an overlap. Amounts are integer minor units, so adjacent tiers `[a,b]`,`[b+1,c]` leave no hidden gap.
  - `tierFor` uses the same inclusive test.
  - `tierRate` makes one half-up rounding of market × (10000 + margin + adjust) through `rateFor`. Fixed price is fixed × (10000 + adjust). The post refuses any tier × method sum past ±5000 (`routes.ts:419`), so `rateFor` cannot throw on a stored offer, and 10000+bps ≥ 5000 > 0 keeps `div` exact.
  - The SQL `finalRateSql` (`routes.ts:217-221`) uses `div` (exact), and a parity test covers huge values (`flexOffers.test.ts:351`).
  - The fixed offer's tiers are forced to margin 0 (`:418`).
- **Take (`apps/api/src/trades/take.ts:251-311`).**
  - The method must be on the offer (400) and available (409).
  - The tier is the one holding the quoted total (409 `out-of-range` in a gap).
  - The tier's `minTrades` applies (409 `not-enough-trades`).
  - The server's own `tierRate` locks, and the client's rate only detects a move.
  - The total at the locked rate must stay inside the same tier (`:283`).
  - The trade's min/max are the tier's, so a later growth stays in the tier whose rate it is.
  - Method, instructions and rate are inserted in the same transaction, with the offer row locked.
  - The `not-enough-trades` refusal carries nothing about the taker or the poster. It only tells the taker the tier's public rule, and the tiers are public on the card anyway. The refusal order (blocked → method → own → … → tier → rule → price) leaks nothing new.

  - Turning a paused offer on never changes the count, because paused already counts. So there is no race between concurrent posts, relists and turn-ons. There is a real-Postgres race test (6 concurrent posts → 2).

## Important

**I1. The payment instructions are locked in the trade but not in the signed terms. A compromised API can show the buyer other payment details, and no device notices.**

- Where: `packages/trade/src/attestation.ts:33` (the deal has `methodId` but nothing for the instructions), `apps/api/src/internal/routes.ts:75-88` `termsOf`, `apps/api/src/trades/view.ts:107`.
- The spec says the method "is locked into the signed trade terms. Each method has its own instructions". The instructions are the one thing in the deal that tells the buyer where the fiat goes, and they reach the buyer only through the unsigned `TradeView`.
- Chat is sealed end to end, so before Task 34 the server could not inject payment details. Now it can.
- Scenario: the API (or anyone who can change its read path or the row before attestation) serves the buyer `paymentInstructions: "IBAN <attacker>"`. The escrow is secured, the buyer pays the attacker, and the seller truthfully says "not received". The XMR is safe, but the buyer's fiat is gone and the dispute cannot show which text the buyer was served.
- Fix (small, and best now, because this is the same `AttestedTerms` key-set change Tasks 9 and 10 must absorb anyway):
  - Add `instructionsSha256` (lower-case hex of SHA-256 over the UTF-8 instructions) to `AttestedTerms.deal`, `isAttestedTerms`, `sameTerms` and `termsOf`.
  - The web `Deal` carries the attested hash.
  - The trade room (Task 35, when it shows the text) displays `paymentInstructions` only when its hash equals the attested one, and otherwise shows a mismatch warning. It also shows the poster the same locked text, so a swap at insert is visible to the person who wrote it.
  - Test: an altered `method_instructions` is caught by the trade-room check. The trigger already stops a change in the database after take.
- If the lead prefers to defer the screen half to Task 35, the hash in the attested terms should still land now, with an Open note in coordination.md.

**I2. The API accepts multi-tier offers today, but the shipped web cannot take them correctly. Every take of such an offer creates a trade that the device then rejects.**

- Where: `apps/api/src/offers/routes.ts:407-414` (the flex shape, any number of tiers, open to any client), `apps/web/src/screens/quote/Quote.tsx:228` (`maxFiatCents: o.maxFiatCents`, the envelope), `apps/web/src/screens/quote/api.ts:45-65` (the quote reads the card's best-tier `pricing` and drops `tiers`).
- Failure scenario: someone posts with curl `tiers: [[100–10000, +5%, 0], [10001–100000, +1%, 0]]`. The card's rate and `pricing` are the best tier's (+1%). A buyer quotes 50 EUR in the web. The quote shows +1%, but the take tiers 50 EUR to +5% and refuses `price-changed` every time (>0.5%). That is a loop.
- Case two: two tiers within 0.5% of each other. The take succeeds and locks tier max 10000, while the device's confirmed deal says the envelope's 100000. The attestation then fails `deal-mismatch` after the trade exists, and the trade dies in setup.
- A third, smaller problem: `not-enough-trades` shows "needs at least 0 completed trades" for a tier with `minTrades: 10`. `takeErrors.ts:46` passes `offer.minTrades`, which is the envelope's lowest rule. The API's 409 at `take.ts:279` sends no `minTrades`.
- A hostile poster can use the first two cases to burn takers' attempts and open-trade slots.
- Fix (until Task 35):
  - Refuse `tiers.length > 1` at `POST /v1/offers` (400, or 409 `not-yet`), with a test.
  - Or, alternatively, have the web quote refuse to open an offer whose card has more than one tier.
  - Either way, put `{ minTrades: tier.minTrades }` in the `not-enough-trades` 409 now and have `takeRefusal` prefer it. That is 2 lines, with a test.
- The coordination note's "Open for Task 35" covers only `maxFiatCents`. It should also name the price and the refusal copy.

## Minor

**M1. The migration test pins every migration after 117.**
- Where: `apps/api/test/flexOffers.test.ts:415`, `toEqual(['117_flex_offers.sql', '306_feedback_form.sql'])`.
- This breaks as soon as `201_activity_log.sql` (staff-core), `701_trade_purge.sql` (Plan 7) or any later migration lands.
- Fix: `expect(applied).toContain('117_flex_offers.sql')` and `expect(applied.every((f) => f >= '117')).toBe(true)`.

**M2. `offer_tiers`/`offer_methods` have no `on delete cascade` and nothing enforcing "written once".**
- Where: `117_flex_offers.sql:12, 26`.
- Plan 7's account delete ("delete … offers") will fail on the foreign key unless it deletes the children first. It also has to delete them, because they hold the payment details.
- Fix: `references offers (id) on delete cascade`. Optionally add a `before update` trigger that refuses changes, to match the comment "written once … never change". Note this for Plan 7 in coordination.md.

**M3. A removed offer keeps its payment instructions forever.**
- Offers are never deleted, only `status = 'removed'`, so `offer_methods.instructions` (IBAN, phone, e-mail) stays at rest indefinitely.
- That goes against the privacy-first retention: trades self-destruct after 30 days, but their source text does not.
- Fix: when an offer becomes `removed` (PATCH, `routes.ts:658-676`), set `instructions = ''` in the same statement or transaction. The trade keeps its own locked copy. Or add it to Plan 7's purge list.

**M4. The cap copy hard-codes "2" and "20", but both are owner settings.**
- Where: `apps/web/src/ui/cantDoCodes.ts:175-178`; `OFFER_CAP_KEYS`.
- After Plan 4's Settings changes `offers_max_per_market`, the popup will state the wrong limit.
- Fix: send `{ perMarket, total }` with the 409 from `assertMayPost` (`routes.ts:471`) and from the bulk route, and interpolate it, keeping the owner's sentence.

**M5. The card's headline rate ignores the tier's trade rule.**
- Where: `routes.ts:257-261` `bestTier` and the list SQL `:711-712` (min/max over all tiers).
- Without `amount`, a newcomer sees, and sorts by, a +1% tier that requires 50 trades, while the price they can actually get is worse. That is bait-like. It applies only to multi-tier offers (see I2).
- Fix (with Task 35): for a signed-in viewer, choose among the tiers whose `minTrades <= completed`. For anonymous viewers, use the `minTrades: 0` tiers, or mark the card.

**M6. Grandfathered over-cap accounts.**
- The old cap allowed 3 per (side, method, currency), so an account can hold, for example, 6 sell/EUR offers after 117. Each one paused can then never be turned on again (`same >= 2`).
- That is acceptable pre-launch, but it should be decided explicitly: either a one-line note in coordination.md, or the migration leaves it as is.
- No action is needed if there is no production data.

**M7. Stale comment.** `117_flex_offers.sql:2-3` says "Numbered after 115 … the next free 1xx"; it is 117 because 116 is Task 38's. Fix the comment.
