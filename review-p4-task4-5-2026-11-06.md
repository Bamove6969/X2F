## Important

**I1 (Task 4). Staff accounts still log in on the trader site, with no login-hours or switch-off check, and that session can manage the staff account's login methods.** Files: `apps/api/src/app.ts:277` (`webauthnLoginRoutes`, kinds `['trader','staff','owner','member']`) and `apps/api/src/auth/pgp.ts:877` (`pgpLoginRoutes`, same kinds). Neither passes an `afterAuthenticate`, so `staffLoginCheck` never runs there. Owner Q19 (2026-11-03, `owner-answers-2026-11-01-claude3.md`): "Staff should have two separate unique logins…", read as staff accounts are staff-only and refused on the trader site. The Global Constraint also says "Staff accounts are separate from trading accounts". Task 4's own note says removing these kinds "is a lead call", and the fix is the unchecked WIP `wip/third-p4-staff-trader-split` @2e11f61.
- Scenario (the class is a login-hours and switch-off bypass, and persistence for a stolen credential): a moderator has Mon-Fri 09:00-17:00 hours, or has been switched off without `disabled_at`. On Saturday they (or someone holding their PGP key and passkey or security key) log in at the trader origin's `/v1/login/pgp` plus `/v1/login/webauthn`. The two-factor rule passes and they get a full `app='trader'` session. Routes guarded by plain `requireSession()` then accept it: `/v1/account/pgp/*` (add a PGP key), `/v1/account/webauthn/*` (add a credential), `DELETE /v1/account/methods/:type/:id`, `/v1/account/login-name`, `/v1/account/sessions` (including ending sessions) and `/v1/confirm/*`. PGP keys work on any origin, so an attacker's PGP key added there works at the staff login once the hours open again. The hours window was meant to limit when stolen credentials work, and this route gets around it.
- Probe (ddb5689): trader-site sessions of an out-of-hours mod and of a switched-off mod both get **200** on GET `/v1/account/me` (kind staff), `/v1/account/sessions` and `/v1/account/methods`. The staff app refuses the same accounts (401 outside-login-hours, 403 account-disabled).
- Fix: land the staff-trader-split (drop `staff` from the trader login kinds, and add a test that a staff login at `/v1/login/{webauthn,pgp}` is refused the same way as an unknown name, with no oracle) together with Task 4 or before it. The owner kind waits on Q19 (see Q1). Until it is fixed, also have Task 7's switch-off set or check something that `requireSession` sees on both apps.

## Minor

Task 4:
- **m1. `staff_members.grants` has no database constraint** (`apps/api/migrations/202_staff.sql:12`). `can()` ignores anything outside `GRANTABLE_PERMS`, so this is defence in depth only. Scenario: a later task reads `grants` directly (a staff list or a NAV builder, say) and trusts a stored `site.control`. Add `check (grants <@ array['tickets.handle']::text[])` and widen it with each grantable perm.
- **m2. The role-kind trigger only guards `staff_members`** (202:23-40). An `update accounts set kind = …` on a staff or owner account is never re-checked against its `staff_members` row. Scenario: a future migration or admin tool turns a staff account into `owner` (or the reverse) and leaves a `mod` row on the owner account, or an `owner` row on a staff account that then skips hours (`withinHours` exempts by role). A matching trigger on `accounts.kind`, or a FK on `(account_id, kind)`, closes it.
- **m3. `X2F_STAFF_ORIGIN` accepts `http:`** (`apps/api/src/staff/main.ts:23`). `__Host-` cookies need Secure, so a plain-http staff origin fails closed (no login works). Still, an operator could "fix" that by renaming the cookie. Allow `http:` only for `localhost`/`127.0.0.1`.
- **m4. The privilege matrix computes expectations with `can()` itself** (`apps/api/test/staff/matrix.test.ts:54`). A wrong grant in `ROLE_PERMS` therefore passes the matrix: only `packages/staff`'s explicit role-by-perm table catches it. The callers also leave out a `member`-kind session, a recovery-level session, a non-empty `grants` and HEAD. Recommend a literal expected-roles column in `routes.ts` (for example `allowed: ['owner']`), plus those callers.
- **m5. The AAGUID allowlist re-check at staff and owner login is still open** (the author's own coordination note). A security key whose AAGUID is withdrawn from the allowlist still logs in to the staff app. Taps do re-check it. Carry it into Tasks 6 and 7 with a tripwire.
- **m6. `staff_members.account_id` and `key_taps.account_id` reference `accounts` with no ON DELETE** (202:9, 203:12). Plan 7 account deletion or purge of a staff account will fail with a FK error. This is acceptable if staff accounts are never deleted, but write that down (or decide it).

Task 5:
- **m7. `taps/start` is perm `self` for every intent kind** (`apps/api/src/staff/keyTap.ts:318-323`). A helper or mod can get a stored, signed `StoredTap` for `{kind:'ruling', step:'approve', …}`, `add-owner-key`, `settings` and so on. Action routes re-derive and check perms, so the API itself is safe. The signed artefact still exists, though, and `StoredTap` is exactly what the moderation gate re-verifies (Task 13). If the gate ever trusts the API's word on who may approve, a mod's self-made "approve" tap would satisfy the two-person rule. Recommend mapping intent kind to the perm required at start (ruling propose → disputes.handle, approve → rulings.approve, emergency-stop/resume → site.control, invites/switch-on/set-role → staff.manage, settings → settings.write, add-owner-key → owner). The gate must still check the signer's role from its own chain of trust. This changes the plan's "perm self": see Q4.
- **m8. A tap's expiry and single use are server-side only** (keyTap.ts:33, 188-193). The signed challenge holds intent and nonce but no time, so a `StoredTap` stays cryptographically valid forever. Unused and expired taps are never purged (the author flagged this). Scenario: a compromised API, or a leaked DB row, re-presents an old unconsumed `ruling approve` tap to the gate. Task 13 must bind a tap to a ruling it has seen once (rulingId uniqueness plus its own first-seen time). Also add the purge of unfinished or expired rows.
- **m9. `consumeKeyTap` does not re-check that the answering credential is still registered and allowlisted** (keyTap.ts:188-195). A key removed as lost (or whose AAGUID is withdrawn) between finish and consume, within 2 minutes, still authorises the action. Add `and exists (select 1 from webauthn_credentials …)` or re-check the allowlist in the UPDATE.
- **m10. The tap is bound to the account, not the session** (keyTap.ts:103, 189). A tap finished on device A can be consumed by session B of the same account. This is low risk because an attacker needs the tapId and a live session. Binding `session_id` is cheap defence in depth.
- **m11. `finishKeyTap` writes `JSON.stringify(response)` to jsonb after the assertion has verified** (keyTap.ts:159). A `\u0000` in an extra field makes Postgres refuse it, so the answer is a 500 and the key counter has already moved. No security impact. Reject NUL in `rec(body.response)` or store the verified fields only.
- **m12. `startKeyTap` has no rate or row limit and no purge** (keyTap.ts:102-105). One staff member can grow `key_taps` without bound. Cap open taps per account (for example 5) and purge rows a day or more old that were never finished or used.

Merge notes (not findings): Task 5 is built on main, not on Task 4. Merging both conflicts in 3 test files only: take Task 4's side of the two tripwire files (it deletes the tripwires Task 5 retargets) and list both 202 and 203 in schema.test.ts. Then mount `keyTapRoutes(app, { guard: requireStaff('self'), accountOf: (req) => req.staff.accountId })` in `buildStaffApi` and add the two rows to `test/staff/routes.ts`. In my trial merge with this resolution, tsc and 155/155 targeted tests pass, so I saw no semantic clash in webauthn.ts or errors.ts.

## What held (attacked, no finding)

- Realm split: a staff token under the trader cookie → 401 on the trader site (probe), a trader token under the staff cookie → 401 (test). `openSession`, `requireSession` and logout all filter on `sessions.app`. Every other session query is by id, or revokes across apps (which is safer).
- Login hours: server clock only (`deps.clock`); checked after the credential verifies at every login step (`completeLogin` runs `afterAuthenticate` on the first and the second factor); checked on every request (the session ends with 401 and is logged exactly once, `endSessionForHours`'s conditional UPDATE); swept every 30 s. Invalid stored hours fail closed. The owner is exempt by DB role, and the DB check forces owner hours to `"any"`. DST and overnight logic is Task 1's (reviewed there); `windowEndsAt` feeds `sessionEndsAt` correctly.
- Key-tap binding: challenge = SHA-256("x2f-keytap-v1\n" + canonicalJson(normalised intent) + "\n" + hex(16-byte random nonce)). `nonce` is unique in the DB. Consume compares the exact canonical string inside the caller's transaction with one conditional UPDATE (`used_at is null and finished_at is not null and started_at > now-2min and intent = $5`), so a parallel consume loses (tested) and a rollback leaves the tap unused (tested). Passkeys and unattested keys are not offered and are refused at finish; the AAGUID is re-checked against the account kind's list. The assertion is verified against the staff origin and RP. The counter must increase (or stay 0).
- Origin/CSRF: unsafe methods need the exact staff origin; JSON-only bodies (text/plain 415); `no-store`, CSP, `no-referrer`.

## Open questions for the lead (not mine to decide)

- **Q1 (owner Q19 follow-up). Does the owner account also get refused on the trader site?** The owner currently uses the trader site for `/v1/owner/price-health` and push subscriptions. *Recommendation:* yes, as claude3 already asked; move those two routes to the staff API (site.control / stats.read).
- **Q2 (owner Q19a). Device approval on the staff app.** The owner said owner and mods "can be logged in on multiple computers… it needs the login approved from the device already logged in". The staff login opens a session with no approval step. *Recommendation:* build Plan 2's device approval for staff-app sessions (a new staff device needs a tap from an already logged-in staff device, or from the owner) as a follow-up task. Until then, record it as not built.
- **Q3. User verification (PIN or biometric) on key taps.** Taps use `userVerification: 'preferred'` and do not require UV for security keys (webauthn.ts:319, 355), so presence alone authorises a ruling, an approval or an emergency stop. *Recommendation:* require UV for `ruling` and `add-owner-key` taps (most FIDO2 keys have PINs), and keep presence alone for the rest.
- **Q4. Should `taps/start` stay perm `self`, as the plan says, or check the intent's perm (m7)?** *Recommendation:* check the intent's perm at start. It is cheap and keeps signed approvals from existing for people who cannot approve.
- **Q5. Does switching a staff member off (Task 7) also set `accounts.disabled_at`?** If not, I1's trader-site bypass stays open for switched-off staff even after the split, wherever a plain `requireSession` exists. *Recommendation:* switch-off ends all sessions on both apps (`endAllSessions`, already in the Task 7 note) and is checked by the staff login (it is).
- **Q6. Keep times for `key_taps`.** *Recommendation:* purge unfinished or unused rows after 24 h. Keep used ruling taps for as long as the ruling record (R36: 90 days from the dispute button) and other used taps 90 days. This is a retention policy question, so it is the owner's (R36 sets no number for this).
