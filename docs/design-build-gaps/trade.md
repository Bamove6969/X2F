## Summary

1. No phone trade screen has the v2 hero (tone wash, chip, key mark, 40/44 number + ring) or the trade stepper; every state is a chip row plus an h1 on the ground, so the "one focal moment" rule fails across TradeFund, BuyerPay, SellerConfirm, SellerRefund, Refunded and all three dispute screens.
2. The trader identity kit is absent: no avatar, online dot, Trusted ring, Vendor tag, stat tiles or badge chips anywhere in trade or dispute; the only counterparty block is the text-only `CounterpartyCard` in the wide chat aside.
3. Owner decisions of 6-7 Nov are not in the build: Q12 (no I've paid or payment details before the escrow is secured), G2 (nudge-first cancel lock, no final-alert steps), G56 ("Cancelling is final" sheet), Q38 parties strip; the build still runs the superseded PayWarning and FinalAlert flows and one-tap buyer cancel.
4. Whole boards are unbuilt: SelfDestruct, TradeTechIssue, Lien-Notice, Ban-Notice, ProofBlur, Appeal-Notice's decided state and completed-trades entry, and the entire staff app (ModQueue, ModCase, StaffOverview/List/Log/Performance/Settings); apps/moderation is a backend only.
5. Shared components drift from the kit: chips are flat surface-raised with three tones only, the key mark is 10px and sits in list rows, the trade header is title-over-code with a border, callouts/warnings are outlined boxes instead of tinted callouts with 18px icons, chat bubbles have no avatar, and `--monero` is #ff7a1a/#f26822 rather than #ff6600.
