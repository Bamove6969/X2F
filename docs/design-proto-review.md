# Prototype review: a first-time trader's walk (8 Nov 2026)

**Build: `wip/lead-proto` @d571748e** (on main 687ae821, Job 1). The file is `apps/web/dist-proto/index.html`, opened via file://. First walk on 27aeed08; every journey re-checked once on d571748e. Compared against the canvas boards (`canvas/project/<Board>.dc.html`, renders `shots/sweep/<Board>.png`) and the flow map of 8 Nov. Viewports: phone 390x844, wide 1280x800. Screenshots are in `scratchpad/proto-shots/<name>.png` (session e5516970); names starting `v2-` are from d571748e, the rest from 27aeed08, on screens the merge didn't touch. Items already in `design-build-gaps/` are skipped unless the prototype makes them worse (marked **worse than listed**).

**Summary: the five worst problems (d571748e)**
1. Once a trade has started, the trader can't find it again. The trade's back arrow and the wide "My trades" link both go to the Sell home, and no list of open trades exists anywhere.
2. The buyer's dispute timing is inverted. "Open dispute" still works during securing, before the buyer has paid (it offers "I paid, but the seller hasn't released"). After "I've paid" it is locked for 2 h, where F9 says 90 min.
3. Deadlines that run out change nothing on screen. At "TIME TO PAY 0 min" the screen still says "Send exactly $412.50" and "I've paid" stays live. When funding reaches "0:00", the buyer is stuck with no cancel and no chat.
4. Posting an offer gives no confirmation. The user lands on "Buy XMR via Zelle" and the new offer appears neither there nor in My offers.
5. The seller's refund path dead-ends in the prototype. The nudge-first lock from Job 1 shows, but nudging never unlocks Cancel (the fake API doesn't record the nudge), and "Give 30 more min" does nothing.
