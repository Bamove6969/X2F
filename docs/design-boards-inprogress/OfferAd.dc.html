Design notes

Draft copy

The public page of one ad, opened from the offers list. Same anatomy on both sides of the coin; the side tweak flips the words, the units of the limits and the button. Showing: {{sideName}}.

  1. Vendor. Avatar with online dot, badges as on Profile, then a stat strip: trades, % positive, star average (1–5, as on Reviews). The chevron opens the profile.
  2. Hero, escrow tone. The chip is the ad's side in the vendor's words (Selling XMR); the line beside it says what that means for you. One hero number: the price for the chosen currency and method, then vs market, then the live market reference.
  3. Currency ▾ and {{ad.methodLabel}} ▾ sit together under the price. Opens on the currency and payment method you searched for. Each method option carries its own adjustment and limit ("PayPal · +1.5% · up to $500"). Changing either updates the price, the limits, the time window, the method line in the terms and the footer maths.
  4. Limits. Selling side in fiat per trade; buying side in XMR with the fiat equivalent under it. A method's own limit narrows the range and the label says so.
  5. All ways {{waysWord}} (kept). A drop-down hides the adjustments until it's opened, so a compact list stays for scanning and comparing. Tapping a row is the same as picking it in the drop-down; selection is the 2px ink border, never orange.
  6. Terms. The vendor's own words, quoted, plus the line for the chosen method. The info callout says every trade is accepted by the vendor.
  7. Escrow row. Small 2-of-3 key mark in escrow tone. This is a new, fourth place for the mark (system v2 §1 lists three); needs sign-off.
  8. Footer. Type either side of the trade ({{toggleNames}}); the other side converts live at the price shown. Out-of-range amounts get an inline error and a disabled button. Button: {{ad.cta}}, not "{{ad.ctaShort}}": the vendor still has to accept, so the button asks rather than promises an instant trade, and still names the side. Message is a tertiary link under it so the pinned footer stays short; the fee line closes it.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov 2026
  • One ad covers every currency the vendor supports. The trader picks currency and payment method from the drop-downs; both are locked in for that trade. Payment-method rules (time to pay, name match…) come with the method.
  • Ad limit: 3 buy + 3 sell ads per vendor.
  • Price tiers and limits are set in one currency and converted at the market rate, shown in the chosen currency with "≈" and the line "Converted from USD at the market rate". Example: $200 – $2,000 shows as ≈ C$277 – C$2,765, ≈ €185 – €1,851, ≈ £158 – £1,580. XMR limits (buying side) need no conversion; only their fiat equivalent moves.

Names, prices and terms are examples. Market prices: USD 167.88, CAD 232.10, EUR 155.40, GBP 132.60.
