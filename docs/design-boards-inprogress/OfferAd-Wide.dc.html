Design notes

Draft copy
  • Same anatomy as the phone board. Showing: {{sideName}}.
  • Opens on the currency and payment method you searched for. Both drop-downs sit in the hero, next to the price they change.
  • The method list stays for scanning: two columns, same selection as the drop-down.
  • The trade panel is the right column, so the amount and the button stay next to the price. The secondary is a full outline button here; on the phone it's a tertiary link to keep the pinned footer short.
  • Button: {{ad.cta}}. The vendor still has to accept.
  • Key mark in the escrow row: a new, fourth placement (system v2 §1). Needs sign-off.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov 2026
  • One ad covers every currency the vendor supports. The trader picks currency and payment method from the drop-downs; both are locked in for that trade. Payment-method rules (time to pay, name match…) come with the method.
  • Ad limit: 3 buy + 3 sell ads per vendor.
  • Price tiers and limits are set in one currency and converted at the market rate, shown in the chosen currency with "≈" and the line "Converted from USD at the market rate". Example: $200 – $2,000 shows as ≈ C$277 – C$2,765, ≈ €185 – €1,851, ≈ £158 – £1,580. XMR limits (buying side) need no conversion; only their fiat equivalent moves.
