# Gift card trades: plain rules (draft, 7 Nov 2026)

Owner F13: keep gift cards; clear, plain rules for both sides, with evidence for disputes. Built from the kind of checks Paxful and LocalMonero asked for (card photo with receipt, code shared only in the trade, quick redemption, recorded redemption as dispute evidence). **Everything below is COPY-UNSIGNED. Rules updated with the owner's 7 Nov answers (G50–G53).**

Who's who: the **card sender** pays with a gift card and gets XMR. The **card receiver** gets the card and sends XMR from escrow.

## Shown on every gift card offer (one short block)

> **Gift card trade.** Use a card bought in the last 30 days from the country on this offer, keep the receipt, and send the code only in this chat. The receiver has 45 minutes to check it and records their screen while they do.

## Card sender: before you tap "I've paid"

1. **Right card.** The card is from the country and currency this offer names (a US card for USD, a Canadian card for CAD).
2. **Bought in the last 30 days, with receipt.** Keep the receipt.
3. **Full value.** The card must hold the full amount written on it. The only exception is a range card (for example a Visa card that says $200 to $500); see below.
4. **Show it, then send it.** Record a short video: log in to the card's balance page, show the card's details and balance, then send the code in this chat. Upload the video here. A photo of the card front, the back with the code and the receipt together is the minimum if a video isn't possible; a range card always needs the video.
5. **Code in this chat only.** Never send it by email, text or another app.
6. **Hands off.** Don't use the card or check its balance after you send the code.

Tick-box before "I've paid":
- [ ] I bought this card in the last 30 days and I have the receipt.
- [ ] It holds the full value written on it (or it's a range card and I recorded the video).
- [ ] I won't use or check this card after sending the code.

## Card receiver: before you release

1. **Check it within 45 minutes** of getting the code: log in, check the balance, redeem it.
2. **Record your screen while you redeem**, from opening the card app or site to the final result. Keep the recording until the trade is over.
3. **Release only when the full amount is in your account.**
4. **Card already used, invalid or short?** Don't release. Open a dispute and add your recording.

Tick-box before "Hold to release":
- [ ] I redeemed the card and the full amount is in my account.

## If there's a dispute: what the moderator looks at

- The sender's photo of card, code and receipt.
- The receiver's screen recording of the redemption.
- The trade chat, with times.

Plain consequence lines (shown in the dispute screen):
- No receipt photo: the sender's case is weaker.
- No screen recording: the receiver's case is weaker.
- A card checked after the 45-minute window: the receiver carries the risk.
- A range card without the sender's video: the sender's case is weaker.

## Decided by the owner (7 Nov, G50–G53)

- 45 minutes for the person holding the escrow to check the card once its details are handed over (G50).
- Video proof allowed and encouraged: log in to the card, show the details, then send (G50).
- Cards bought within the last 30 days (G51).
- Full written value only; range cards (e.g. Visa $200–$500) may be partial and always need the video (G52).
- Brand list: goes to the owner to approve, add or remove (G53). Design's draft for that list: Amazon, Apple, Google Play, Steam, Visa prepaid, Mastercard prepaid, Walmart, Target, eBay, Razer Gold; legal check per brand (Q30).
