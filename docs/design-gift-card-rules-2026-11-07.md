# Gift card trades: plain rules (draft, 7 Nov 2026)

Owner F13: keep gift cards; clear, plain rules for both sides, with evidence for disputes. Built from the kind of checks Paxful and LocalMonero asked for (card photo with receipt, code shared only in the trade, quick redemption, recorded redemption as dispute evidence). **Everything below is COPY-UNSIGNED and the numbers (marked ⓘ) are rule calls for the owner.**

Who's who: the **card sender** pays with a gift card and gets XMR. The **card receiver** gets the card and sends XMR from escrow.

## Shown on every gift card offer (one short block)

> **Gift card trade.** Use a new card from the country on this offer, keep the receipt, and send the code only in this chat. The receiver redeems it within 30 minutes ⓘ and records their screen while they do.

## Card sender: before you tap "I've paid"

1. **Right card.** The card is from the country and currency this offer names (a US card for USD, a Canadian card for CAD).
2. **New card, with receipt.** Buy it new from a store or the brand's own site. Keep the receipt.
3. **One photo, three things.** Card front, the back with the code showing, and the receipt, together in one photo. The receipt must show the store, date, time and amount.
4. **Code in this chat only.** Never send it by email, text or another app.
5. **Hands off.** Don't use the card or check its balance after you send the code.

Tick-box before "I've paid":
- [ ] I bought this card new and I have the receipt.
- [ ] I won't use or check this card after sending the code.

## Card receiver: before you release

1. **Redeem within 30 minutes** ⓘ of getting the code.
2. **Record your screen while you redeem**, from opening the card app or site to the final result. Keep the recording until the trade is over.
3. **Release only when the full amount is in your account.**
4. **Card already used, invalid or short?** Don't release. Open a dispute and add your recording.

Tick-box before "Hold to release":
- [ ] I redeemed the card and the full amount is in my account.

## If there's a dispute: what the moderator looks at

- The sender's photo of card, code and receipt.
- The receiver's screen recording of the redemption.
- The trade chat, with times.

Plain consequence lines (shown in the dispute screen):
- No receipt photo: the sender's case is weaker.
- No screen recording: the receiver's case is weaker.
- A card redeemed after the 30-minute window ⓘ: the receiver carries the risk.

## For the owner to decide (ⓘ)

| Rule | Design's recommendation |
|---|---|
| Redemption window | 30 minutes after the code is sent |
| Card age | Bought within the last 7 days |
| Accepted brands | A short list at launch (for example Amazon, Apple, Google Play, Steam, Visa prepaid), with legal check per brand (owner Q30) |
| Partial-balance cards | Not allowed: the card must carry the full trade amount |
