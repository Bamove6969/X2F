Passes. SSRF (focus 1): `canonicalEndpoint` (push/endpoint.ts:33-47) accepts only the canonical WHATWG href, https, no userinfo, port or fragment, plain DNS labels, on a 5-domain push-service allowlist. It is checked at subscribe time and again before each send (send.ts:79, :116). The connection goes through an `https.Agent` whose `lookup` (endpoint.ts:88-100) resolves every address and refuses the host if any one is outside public unicast. That covers RFC 6890 v4, everything outside 2000::/3 (so IPv4-mapped, NAT64, link-local, ULA and loopback), and Teredo, 6to4 and documentation ranges inside it. The address that was checked is the address connected to, so DNS is resolved once per connection and a rebind cannot slip past. Test push.test.ts:413 proves the agent's lookup is the one used. web-push makes a single `https.request`, which follows no redirect (a 3xx comes back as a WebPushError status), and `proxy: undefined` disables the proxy path. Caps: 10 subscriptions per account, with a lock on the account row, insert, then trim (routes.ts:80-93). Endpoints are at most 1024 characters, the timeout is 10 s and the TTL is 15 min. The response body size is not capped, but only allowlisted hosts are contacted. Ownership (focus 2): DELETE is scoped by `account_id`. Re-posting an endpoint moves it to the last account to post it, which needs the endpoint's capability URL and only drops the other account's pushes. A POST racing a logout leaves a row whose session is revoked, and sendPush deletes such rows before selecting (send.ts:101-105). SSE caps (focus 3): 5 per account and 2000 per process, checked synchronously in `attach`, with 429 `busy` before hijack (tested). The client backs off from 1 s up to 60 s. Stream end (focus 4): logout, re-login over a cookie, a single revoke, password set and method removal (after commit), and endOtherSessions/endAllSessions all close streams through the bus. The stream re-checks its session after attaching (closes the TOCTOU window). The migration 105 trigger drops push rows on every revoke path, and the cascade covers account deletion. Payloads (focus 5): only the two fixed strings (send.ts:14-17), and the service worker maps anything else to the trade text. SSE frames are JSON with names from the public-name column. final_alert_delivered (focus 6): nothing server-side sets it. An open stream or a 201 never counts. The final alert is published and pushed once, after commit. A failed push is not retried, so the column stays null and moderation escalates (fails closed). Deviation (focus 7): the "Lead-approved deviation" is plan Revision 2026-10-25 (plan-3-trading.md:5-11). It replaces the Task 19 interface's `final_alert_delivered = delivered || pushAccepted` with "true only from the buyer's seen-receipt, null otherwise, Task 10 treats null as false". It is recorded in the plan (revision plus corrected Task 19 lines) and in docs/coordination.md (2026-10-25 bullet), alongside the one-process bus and the global-cap DoS limits.

## Important
1. apps/api/src/events/routes.ts:37 + apps/api/src/auth/sessions.ts:236-249 + apps/web/src/api/events.ts:82-88: an open tab now keeps a session alive forever, which defeats the inactivity logout timer (spec 6.3: "how long they stay logged in while inactive", 2-12 h). `GET /v1/events` runs plain `requireTrader`, and that call writes `last_seen_at` and pushes `expires_at` forward. The server ends every stream after `maxStreamMs` (10 min), and the browser reconnects at once, so an idle tab touches the session every 10 min. The 429/401 retry loop also passes through `requireSession` first. Before this task the web app made no background requests. Trigger: a trader on a shared or office computer leaves X2F open with the timer at 2 h and walks away. The session is still live days later, up to the 30-day absolute cap. Fix: add a `requireSession({ touch: false })` option (read-only check, same refusals) and use it for `/v1/events`. Also end each stream at the session's `expires_at` (`lifetime = min(maxStreamMs, expires_at - now)`), so an idle-expired session stops streaming at its deadline rather than up to 10 min later. Add a test showing that a stream reconnecting past the idle deadline gets 401.
2. apps/web/src/App.tsx:65-66 + apps/web/src/api/events.ts:42-45: the web app sends the final-alert seen-receipt without showing anything. `LiveAccepted` is mounted for every signed-in trader. Whenever a `{type:'trade', state:'refund_requested'}` event reaches any open tab, it POSTs `/v1/trades/:code/final-alert-seen`, even when the tab is hidden, minimised or on another device. No component renders the spec 2.3 alert ("The seller is cancelling. If you already paid, tap I've paid now."). The receipt therefore turns "a stream was open" into "seen", which is exactly what Revision 2026-10-25 rules out, and Task 10 will auto-refund on it. Trigger: the buyer paid but forgot "I've paid", left X2F open in a background tab at home and is out with their phone. The seller taps Cancel & get refund, the hidden tab posts the receipt at once, and 15 min later the moderator key auto-refunds the seller with no escalation. Fix: post the receipt only after the alert has really been shown. Two ways: (a) when `document.visibilityState === 'visible'`, render a final-alert sheet with the spec text and post when it is displayed or acknowledged; (b) when hidden, show a `Notification` (as Task 26 does for nudges) and post after `show` fires, or leave the receipt to the service worker push. Add a test that a hidden tab posts no receipt.
3. apps/api/src/push/routes.ts:67 and push/send.ts:7 (missing hook): the owner's price-health phone push (spec 11.6, "through the owner's in-app alerts and a phone push"; docs/coordination.md, 2026-10-24 second: "Task 19 hooks the push in where `main.ts` passes `onAlert`") is not built. Push routes are `kinds: ['trader']`, so an owner cannot subscribe, and there is no owner/alert push kind. The branch's base (27ea8b9) predates Task 3, so a plain merge leaves `main.ts`'s `onAlert` writing in-app alerts only. Trigger: a price source fails or market prices pause while the owner has no tab open, and the owner is not told until they next log in. Fix: allow `owner` (and later staff) to subscribe, add a third fixed payload (for example "X2F needs your attention"), and in `main.ts` `onAlert` call `push.send(ownerId, 'alert')` after `addOwnerAlert`. Alternatively, have the lead record it in coordination.md as a named follow-up with an owner.
4. apps/web/src/screens/AcceptedSheet.tsx:68: the sheet pre-selects the first usable wallet (`setPicked(usable[0]?.id ?? null)`), so one tap on "Use this wallet" commits it. That is a default payout wallet, contrary to OD-11 / X-2 ("No default payout wallet; always ask", plan:112, :162), and contrary to the component's own comment at :38. Trigger: a poster who keeps separate wallets for funds of different origins (spec 6.3 "Address book") taps through the sheet, and the trade is bound to whichever wallet the API listed first. Fix: start with `picked = null` (button disabled until a radio is chosen), and add a test that the button is disabled until a pick.

## Minor
5. apps/api/src/trades/routes.ts:94-100: check-then-act. The role, state and `finalAlertAt` are read without a lock, then `update trades set final_alert_delivered = true where code = $1` runs unconditionally. Trigger: the buyer's mark-paid commits between the read and the update, and a paid trade ends up flagged. Today that is harmless, but the column then means nothing. Fix: put the conditions in the UPDATE (`and buyer_account_id = $2 and state = 'refund_requested' and final_alert_at is not null`) and answer from `rowCount`.
6. apps/api/migrations/105_push.sql:38: the receipt is a boolean with no time. Trigger: a push held by the push service for 14 min (TTL 15 min) is shown, and the service worker posts at minute 14. Task 10 sees `true` and auto-refunds one minute later, though the buyer had 1 minute, not 15. Fix: store `final_alert_seen_at timestamptz` (set once, `coalesce`) and let Task 10 require `now >= seen_at + 15 min` or escalate. Record this in the Task 10 notes.
7. apps/web/public/sw.js:29: `tag: 'x2f'` without `renotify: true`. Under the Notifications standard, replacing a same-tag notification skips the alert steps (no sound or vibration), yet the receipt (:32-34) is still posted. Trigger: an unread nudge notification is in the tray when the final-alert push arrives, so the alert replaces it silently and the trade is marked seen. Fix: `renotify: true`, or a separate tag for trade pushes.
8. apps/api/src/events/tradePublisher.ts:17-32 via trades/store.ts:320: delivery, including the web-push round-trip (10 s socket timeout per call), is awaited inside the actor's HTTP request. Trigger: the seller's `request-refund` response time shows whether the buyer has push subscriptions (and for other events whether the counterparty has a stream open), and a slow push service stalls the actor's request. Fix: deliver in `setImmediate` after commit, as nudges do (nudge/routes.ts `deliver`).
9. apps/api/src/events/bus.ts:58: the cap is per account only. Five tabs, or half-open sockets from a phone that changed networks (kept until the TCP timeout or `maxStreamMs`), lock the account's other devices out with 429 for up to 10 min. Fix: when the account is at its cap, evict that account's oldest stream instead of refusing, or add a per-session sub-cap. Either way, log 429 counts only (no ids).
10. docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-15-plan-3-trading.md:487 (Task 10) still reads "reports the final alert delivered (SSE open or push accepted, Task 19)". The revision only corrected the Task 19 lines. Fix: correct the Task 10 line to "buyer's seen-receipt; null = false → escalate" so the next worker does not build the old rule.
