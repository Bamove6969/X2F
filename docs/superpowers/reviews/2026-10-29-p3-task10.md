## Verdict: CHANGES REQUIRED (3 Important, 7 Minor)

The core guard is sound: the only `settle()` caller in apps/moderation is `desk.ts` (pinned by a static test). It settles only `payoutsFor('auto-refund' | 'buyer-cancel')`, which is one payout of the whole balance to `sellerRefund`. That address is the one the seller's own identity signed on the signals channel, and it is adopted only when it equals the attested `sellerRefund` and deal. The API's `seller_refund` is never read. Signals are opened with the channel's persisted ReplayGuard, are bound to the channel (a different HKDF channel id per kind, so no cross-channel replay), are checked against `SIGNAL_SENDERS` using the signed `from`, and are saved before any action or ack. `trade.ack` is sealed to the sender alone. The auto-refund requires all of these: the API in `refund_requested`, no `trade.paid` ever opened, the shadow in `refund_requested` with `finalAlertAt > payBy`, `now >= shadow.finalAlertAt + 15 min`, a seen-receipt with `now >= seen + 15 min`, and `balance == unlocked > 0`. The API can only delay. Emergency stop (`trading_paused`) does not touch refunds, which matches spec §450 ("trades already in escrow can still be … refunded"). I1 and I2 are about timing: when the mod's windows end compared with the API's `mark-paid` window. I3 is the owner's option-B rule, which the desk does not follow yet.

## Important

**I1. The mod can sign the no-show refund while the API still lets the buyer mark paid, after maintenance.** `apps/moderation/src/desk.ts:293-294`, `apps/moderation/src/autoRefund.ts:67-82`.
The shadow pauses and resumes only from what the mod itself sees on the watch list (`paused`), on its own clock. Two consequences:
- `resume` shifts a window only if it was still open when the *shadow* paused (machine.ts `opens()`).
- The receipt time `seen` is never shifted.

The API instead shifts its own `finalAlertAt` by its own pause, and its `mark-paid` stays open until that shifted `finalAlertAt + 15 min`. Three concrete failures:
1. **Mod down across the pause start (planned maintenance, which is exactly when services are restarted).**
   - At 12:00 the seller requests a refund. The buyer sees the alert at 12:01. Maintenance runs 12:05 to 13:00 (the API pauses the trade at 12:05, with 10 min left). The moderation service is upgraded and is down 12:06 to 12:50.
   - At 12:50 the mod sees `paused` and pauses the shadow. The shadow's window ended at 12:15, so the pause does not shift it.
   - At 13:00 maintenance ends. The API window now ends at 13:10. The mod's next pass (about 13:00:15) sees `paused: false`, finds shadow F+15 and seen+15 both passed, and signs the refund. The buyer still had 10 minutes to tap I've paid.
2. **A seller who sends `trade.refund-request` before posting the API's `request-refund`.**
   - The early signal is kept in `pending` and applied at the shadow's own payBy. The shadow is then `refund_requested` long before the API.
   - Any maintenance during the API's later final alert is lost on the shadow (its window is already over), and `seen` is not shifted. The mod signs within one pass of the resume.
3. **Without any outage:** the mod sees the pause up to one pull plus one pass late (author's "accepted side effect"). The shadow's shift can come out up to about 15 s shorter than the API's.

Fix (cheap, and consistent with "the API only brakes"):
- Put the API's pause-shifted `finalAlertAt` (and `pausedAt`) in the watch item.
- In `autoRefundStep`, wait until `max(shadow.finalAlertAt, api.finalAlertAt, seen) + 15 min + margin`. The margin should be at least one watch pass plus a clock-skew allowance, for example 60 s.

This also covers M1 (clock skew). Tests to add: (a) a mod outage across the pause start; (b) an early refund-request signal followed by maintenance during the API's alert; in both, nothing is signed before the API's shifted F+15.

**I2. Decision (1), "receipt younger than 15 min → wait": the buyer's 15 minutes from the receipt are not real.** `autoRefund.ts:79-82` against `packages/trade/src/machine.ts:444-447`.
- The mod waits until `seen + 15 min`. The API refuses `mark-paid` as `too-late` from `finalAlertAt + 15 min`, counted from the alert, not the receipt.
- So a buyer whose push sat with the push service for 14 min 50 s (within the 15-min TTL that the plan gives as the reason to count from the receipt) sees the alert at F+14:50, has 10 s to tap I've paid, and the moderator then refunds automatically at F+29:50.
- The plan's intent (15 min from the receipt, else a person decides) is not met.
- claude2 raised this correctly as an open decision. Because the built default is the least safe choice for the buyer, I rate it Important: it needs the lead's call before landing.
- Recommendation: option (b). Escalate (`alert-unseen`, or a new `alert-late`) when `seen > finalAlertAt + δ`, with δ of 1–2 min. It is Task 10 only and fails safe. Option (c), the API counting `mark-paid` from the receipt, is better UX but changes Task 2 and Task 19.
- If the lead keeps (a), this becomes Minor, and a code comment should say that the receipt wait adds nothing.

**I3. Option B (owner, decided): the moderator still co-signs a buyer cancel after "I've paid".** `apps/moderation/src/desk.ts:199-204`, `:310-317`; `apps/moderation/src/buyerCancel.ts:25`.
- `trade.cancel` sets `cancelSeen: true` even when the shadow refuses `buyer-cancel`. `buyerCancelStep` reads only `cancelSeen`, so the mod co-signs the buyer-built refund in any state and never looks at `paidSeen` or the shadow.
- Failure scenario: the buyer marks paid (the mod opens `trade.paid`, `paidSeen` is true). Later a `trade.cancel` from the buyer's identity arrives: an accidental tap, an old client, or a client that sends the signal although the API (after Task 39) refuses `buyer-cancel` in `paid`. Once the escrow is unlocked the mod signs at once, and the seller gets both the fiat and the XMR. The owner's rule says no cancel of any kind after "I've paid", and the main-line plan text for B requires this exact refusal.
- The same path signs in `settling` (a release in progress), `disputed`, `lapsed` and closed states, all of which the machine refuses (machine.ts:144 `BUYER_CANCEL_SETTLE`). It pays only the seller, so the harm falls on the buyer, but it is not what B describes.
- Fix:
  - `buyerCancelStep` refuses when `paidSeen` (check it at every step, not only when the cancel arrives, so a `trade.paid` opened while the cancel waits for coins to unlock still blocks it).
  - Do not count a `trade.cancel` the shadow refused as `wrong-state` (keep the cancel-in-`setup` / nothing-received case, which closes the shadow).
  - As an extra brake, refuse while the API shows `paid`.
- Tests (from the plan's Task 39 Step 0): mod opened `trade.paid`, then `trade.cancel` → nothing signed. Also `trade.cancel`, then `trade.paid` before unlock → nothing signed. And `trade.cancel` in `settling` (release) → nothing signed.
- Decision (4) ("still works after an escalation") needs re-confirming under B: B's text names only `paid`, but after a system escalation from `refund_requested` the buyer had not marked paid.

## Minor

- **M1. Clock skew between the API and moderation.** `seen` is an API-clock time compared with the mod's `now` (`autoRefund.ts:82`). In I1's case 2 the shadow gives no protection, so a mod clock ahead of the API by more than `seen − F` (often 1–2 s when the buyer has the app open) signs before the API closes `mark-paid`. The I1 margin fixes this.
- **M2. Desk facts can be lost, and a missing desk means no escalation either.**
  - `watcher.ts:342-348`: when `desk.step` throws (a DB blip), the WatchState is still saved. That pass's `chain` or `settled` reports never reach the shadow, which then stays in `securing` or `funding`. The refund then only escalates (`refund-unverified`); this fails safe.
  - `watcher.ts:260-265`: `openDesk` failing at party start is never retried in that process. With no desk there is no auto-refund, no buyer-cancel and **no `refund-unverified` either**: the API sits in `refund_requested` with nobody alerted until a restart.
  - Deploy note: trades already in flight when Task 10 first deploys get a fresh shadow (`takenAt` = deploy time) with no chain facts, so every one of them escalates instead of auto-refunding.
  - Suggestion: feed the shadow a `chain` event from the pass's own `facts.funds` every pass (not only the reported deltas), and retry `openDesk` on later passes.
- **M3. A cancel that cannot proceed blocks every escalation.** `desk.ts:310-319`: with `cancelSeen` and no seller terms (`bc.action === 'wait'`), `autoRefundStep` (and with it the `refund-unverified` fallback) is skipped forever, so the trade hangs with nobody alerted.
- **M4. An armed refund is retried after the trade closed.** `desk.ts:299-307` and `248-260`: after the armed refund's sweep (or any other sweep), late coins make `balance == unlocked > 0` again. The desk then calls `settle` with a new outcome every 15 s for the 30-day watch. The party refuses (`committed`), so nothing is signed, but the log gets `*-signing` and `*-settle-failed` every pass. Clear `armed` (or stop) once the shadow or the API is closed or the sweep was shown. Late funds must go to a person (P3-I6), never to the guard.
- **M5. `pending` is never de-duplicated.** `desk.ts:211`: a seller sending many `trade.extend` or `trade.refund-request` before the shadow's payBy grows the record (saved every frame) without limit. Keep at most one of each.
- **M6. The plan's regtest tests are not built.** Task 10's files say "(regtest)". The moderation tests are hermetic with a recording party. "A seller-built refund paying another address is never co-signed, nothing broadcast" and "a buyer-built set paying the buyer → never co-signed" are proved only one level down (`packages/escrow/test/engine.test.ts`, verify.test.ts) plus the pure `buyerCancelStep`. This is acceptable if Task 31 is told to cover both end-to-end; record that in coordination.md.
- **M7. Decision (2).** Escalate at once when `paidSeen` while the API shows `refund_requested` (a definite conflict, so a person sooner). Keep the 60 min only for the other mismatches. The 60 min also keeps running through a pause (the author's note); with I1 fixed, consider not counting while paused.
