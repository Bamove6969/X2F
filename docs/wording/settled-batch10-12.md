# Settled wording, Wording Room batches 10, 11 and 12

Design, 12 Nov 2026. On 10 Nov the owner ruled that wording questions go to design and design's answers count as signed. These are the final words. The owner's own answers are kept exactly.

**Counts (273 questions settled):** kept 183 · changed 89 · owner 1. 
Also recorded: 15 owner answers, kept as he gave them (w10-1 to w10-8, w10-22 to w10-28). Skipped: w10-9 to w10-21 (removed by the owner; the payment field lists are being rebuilt).

**How to read a row.** Each quoted string is one line on screen, in the order given. A note in brackets, not quoted, is an instruction (for example "remove"). "Location" is where the string's current text lives on `origin/main` (6ebed537), found by `git grep`; "not found on main" means the line is not built yet, or main has already replaced it. A row that changes a sample name or date only is marked kept.

**Rules applied everywhere.**
- One price wording: "0.4% above market" / "0.5% below market" / "at market". "Vendor markup" becomes "the seller's price".
- Labels hold no colon (a test checks this). Three accessible labels lost theirs: w10-79, w10-88 and w12-41.
- "Trading hold" is now the moderator's freeze. Open disputes never block trading (lead, 12 Nov), so every "trading hold while a dispute is open" line now uses the AccountFrozen words: "A moderator froze trading on your account" / "Trading frozen". See w11-110 to w11-113, w12-21, w12-23, w12-26 and w12-63. The batch 7 notice title becomes "Your offers are paused while trading is frozen".
- The recovery-code hold has one wording in every place it appears. w12-64, w12-65, w12-66 and w11-114 repeat w10-104 and w10-105.
- Cancel and refund results say "the seller" or "the buyer", never a name (owner, 29 Oct).
- A weak login is "just a username and password" (Q14). A PGP key counts as strong (Q81). A trader's password plus authenticator code counts as strong for approving changes (G34), but it does not end a recovery hold.
- Behaviour owed by the build (the wording assumes it lands): D-070, moderators with a username and password (w11-1, w11-12, w11-22, w11-23); S-104, an invite from a weak login (w10-106, w12-67); S-054, members may use any login method (w11-96, w12-43); p11-36, the person makes their own new recovery code (w10-97 to w10-101). Until each one lands, the line on main stays as it is.
