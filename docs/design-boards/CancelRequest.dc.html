The confirm step is the buyer's screen; the side tweak changes the returning and cancelled steps.
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G2) Who can cancel, and when
  • Before escrow is funded: either side can cancel at once, no reason needed.
  • Seller, after escrow, if the buyer hasn't been in touch: once the time to pay has ended with no I've paid, Cancel is there at once and the escrowed XMR goes back to the seller's wallet. No nudge is needed (p6-1, 8 Nov, replaces the 7 Nov nudge rule).
  • Buyer, after escrow: can cancel. That triggers a full refund to the seller, and the trade stays open until the refund has all 10 confirmations.
