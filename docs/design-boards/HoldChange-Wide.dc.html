Decided · owner, 2 Nov Submitting changes from a username-and-password session no longer logs anyone out. The account keeps working as normal; only the submitted changes wait 24 h before they take effect.
  • No logout for you or team members, and trades are not cancelled or put on hold.
  • While changes wait, a banner stays at the top of the app with the count, the date they take effect and Review changes.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov Anyone logged in to the account, even with just a username and password, can cancel the waiting changes. Cancel these changes sits next to Review changes in the banner, with a short confirm. Try it on the submitted step.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov The account owner is told at once on every channel they set up (push, secure Jabber and others), listing each change.
OPEN · owner to choose If someone logs in with just a username and password and changes payout wallets or login methods, should it apply on its own after 24 hours? Risk raised with the owner: if a thief has the username and password, and the owner only has a username and password too, the owner has no passkey, security key or PGP key to end the hold, so the thief's new wallet would apply automatically.
