Design notes

Showing: {{stepName}} · {{loginName}}

Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G29, G5, G36) All pending changes are submitted together from one review, showing each one before and after.
  • Grouped by Login methods, Wallets and Team. Up to 20 changes can wait at once (G36).
  • Username-and-password login: removing a wallet or a team member takes effect at once (wallet unusable, access gone) and becomes final after 24 h. Everything else applies after 24 h (G5).
  • Passkey, security key or PGP key login: no wait, but the same before-and-after review (G29).
  • Team members can't see the owner's waiting changes (G31).
Decided · owner, 2 Nov Submitting changes from a username-and-password session no longer logs anyone out. The account keeps working as normal; only the submitted changes wait 24 h before they take effect.
  • No logout for you or team members, and trades are not cancelled or put on hold.
  • While changes wait, a banner stays at the top of the app with the count, the date they take effect and Review changes.
  • Since 7 Nov (G5), removals are the exception: they take effect at once.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov Anyone logged in to the account, even with just a username and password, can cancel the waiting changes. Cancel these changes sits next to Review changes in the banner, with a short confirm. Try it on the submitted step.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov The account owner is told at once on every channel they set up (push, secure Jabber and others), listing each change.
OPEN · owner to supply Moderator contact: the Matrix or Telegram address the warning should show.
  • Matrix address
  • Telegram address
  • Both
Until then the warning says only "or message a moderator".
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G57, G58) A lead who logs in with a passkey, security key or PGP key while changes wait sees them and decides: Approve now, Make changes or Cancel all changes.
  • The approve step: a "Changes waiting for you" hero with the countdown (23 h 12 min) and "They apply on their own when the time is up", then the same before-and-after list as the review, then the three actions. Approve now is the one primary; Cancel all changes is the danger tertiary and uses the cancel confirm.
  • The waiting changes may be the lead's own (made with a username and password) or a team member's with limited rights. A strong login no longer throws them away on its own (this replaces the earlier "thrown away" rule).
  • Team rows (G57): removing a member reads "Logged out now · removed in 24 h"; every other team change reads "Applies after 24 h". Only the lead can remove team members.
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (g65) One view lists every waiting change and its effect. Each change has a tick; cancelling is Cancel all changes or unticking single changes.
  • Each row reads change → effect: before, after, then the effect line. Held changes carry the countdown (23 h 12 min); the hero shows how many are ticked.
  • Every change starts ticked. An unticked change never applies, so nothing needs restoring; the row dims and reads "Won't apply". Approve now puts through only the ticked ones.
  • Cancel all changes (renamed from Cancel all) drops every change after the short confirm; the confirm no longer talks about restoring anything.
  • Strong login (tweak instant): the same view, but every effect reads "Applies now", the countdown is replaced by "Applies now" and the primary is Apply changes.
Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q14)Changes apply on their own after the 24 h wait
  • Anyone logged in can cancel them during the wait; the owner is alerted on every channel.
  • Never say "password only": the weakest login is a username and password.
