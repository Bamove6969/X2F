Design notes

Header: logo D (owner, 8 Nov, page 8); in the wide rail the page name sits under the logo so it never cuts off (comment #2).

Decided · sent to the build, 2 Nov
  • Offer limit (owner, 2 Nov): 3 buying and 3 selling offers per vendor; one offer covers all the currencies it lists (shown on each card, e.g. USD · CAD). The limit sentence is draft copy.
  • A ticked offer row gets a 2px ink ring on --surface-raised.
  • During a moderator trading hold, every offer shows a caution chip "Paused · trading hold", the switches (and Turn on / Turn off) are disabled, and one caution notice explains the hold. Preview with the hold tweak. Owner, 2 Nov: a hold has no end time, so the notice shows no date; open disputes never pause trading, only ignoring a dispute or a ruling does.
  • The current nav item is "Post offer" (offer boards live under it): --surface-raised with ink text and a 2px ink inset line.
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (F4)
  • Every offer shows its currencies as small chips (flag and code). A currency with a market rate is a plain chip.
  • One currency has no rate: its chip is struck through, with a caution outline, a signal-off icon and the words “Not available” (O-022, 9 Nov), and one caution line under the chips says so. The other currencies stay live.
  • No currency has a rate: the offer can’t go live. It shows a caution chip “Can’t go live” (was “Not live · no rate”, O-022, 9 Nov) and one line; the switch stays on and the rest of the card dims to 70%.
  • Nothing for the vendor to do: when the rate comes back, the currency (or the offer) goes live by itself; nothing for the vendor to do. X2F’s owner is notified when a currency drops or comes back (owner Q35).
  • Preview with the rate tweak (all, one-missing, none). The chip label and the two caution lines are draft copy.
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (O-023, F5): design's call, 9 Nov The screen stays “My offers”, as in the rail and on every board that links here. Picked over My listings / My ads / My posts: an “offer” is the word used everywhere else in X2F (Post an offer, BuyOffers, OfferAd).

Craft pass, 7 Nov: three stat tiles under the title (on, selling, buying); each row leads with a 40 px side tile (arrow up-right = selling, down-left = buying; on a ticked row the tile steps to --surface); the offer column is wider so the side line stops wrapping; Edit gets a chevron.
