Design notes

Header: logo D (owner, 8 Nov, page 8); in the wide rail the page name sits under the logo so it never cuts off (comment #2).

Showing: {{stepName}}

Craft pass, 7 Nov: the two privacy promises are one surface block with 32 icon tiles (eye-off in escrow, lock in ink) and an inset divider, instead of two loose lines on the ground. Wording unchanged.

Decided · owner, 2 Nov Proof is cleaned on the device before anything is sent.
  • Photo metadata (EXIF: location, device) is stripped on the phone.
  • Likely private details are pre-blurred: account and card numbers, full names, addresses, phone numbers and other contact details.
  • The buyer reviews, adds blur where needed, then sends. Auto-blur is best-effort, and the screen says so.
  • The seller only gets the blurred version. A moderator sees the unblurred original only in a dispute.
  • 8 Nov: in trader-facing text this is "our privacy tool", and what it covers is "hidden". "Add blur" and "Remove blur" keep the word blur, because they are the uploader's own boxes.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov On upload, an encrypted copy of the exact original is kept automatically, so a moderator sees the same image in a dispute. Traders are never asked to re-send.
  • No dispute: deleted 48 h after the trade ends.
  • Dispute: kept until the ruling's 48 h appeal window closes, then deleted.
  • Traders are told, in a permanent line (lock icon) under the privacy tool line: "We keep an encrypted original copy that only a moderator can open, and only if there's a dispute. It's deleted automatically when the trade is over."
Decided · owner, 2 Nov X2F builds a database of blur templates to improve automatic blurring. A template is the layout of a payment app's proof screen with the private areas blanked (nothing underneath). No real proofs are stored.
Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q50) The trade code, amount and time are never blurred. Names and account numbers can be blurred.
  • Blur templates and automatic blur never blank these three fields.
  • Owner: "Trade code, amount, and time should never be blurred… Names and account numbers can be blurred."
  • If the uploader's own blur covers the amount or memo, the 2 Nov "Warn, don't block" rule still applies.
Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q42, Q43) Two checks before sending.
  • Q43, amount not detected: the buyer can still post the proof. Owner: "post anyways".
  • Q42, screenshot date older than the trade: the buyer is asked for a fresh screenshot. The old one is not sent.
Design note The amount and memo are never auto-blurred. The seller needs both to find and match the payment, and the line under the image says so.
  • Blur box: frosted fill, 1px dashed escrow outline, small Auto tag (Added for boxes the buyer draws).
  • Preview as seller hides outlines and tags and turns the other tools off, so the image is exactly what is sent.
  • Each blur is a button for screen readers; its tap area grows to 44 px. Add blur: tap a line or drag a box.
  • Wide: the Hidden details list is the keyboard path. Every blur can be shown or removed from there.
  • The receipt is a CSS stand-in for the buyer's own screenshot; the names and numbers on it are examples.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov Add only. The uploader can add blur but never remove an automatic blur. The automatic blur never hides the amount.
