Trading

  • Each trade gets a fresh 2-of-3 escrow. You, the other trader and X2F each hold one key, and any two keys can move the coins.

    X2F holds only its one key, so it can never move the coins on its own.

    In the app you see three steps: Fund, I've paid, Release.

  • Trade takes an offer. The offer's poster is told you accepted, and if you're the buyer you pick which of your saved wallets gets paid.

    The seller then has 30 min to fund the escrow from their own wallet. The price locks at that moment.

  • Only the person who posted the offer pays a fee: 1% of the trade, taken when the trade completes.

    Taking an offer costs nothing. Cancelled and refunded trades pay no fee.

  • The buyer has 90 min to pay once the escrow is secured. If they never tap I've paid, the seller can tap Cancel & get refund after that window.

    The buyer gets a final 15 min alert. If they stay silent, the coins go back to the seller's saved wallet. No moderator is involved.

  • A buyer can cancel alone at any time before tapping I've paid. The coins go back to the seller's saved wallet.

    Once the escrow is funded, the seller can't cancel alone. Once you've tapped I've paid, neither can you.

  • If the two of you can't agree, either side can open one. A moderator reads the chat and the proofs, then signs with the side that is right.

    Each dispute has a published deadline. If you disagree with the ruling, you can appeal to a senior moderator within 48 h.

Logging in

  • There is no password to remember and nothing to steal: X2F holds only your public key.

    Every login is the same three steps: copy the message, decrypt it in your PGP app, paste the code. Each code works once and expires in 5 min.

  • Kleopatra on Windows and Linux, GPG Suite on Mac, OpenKeychain on Android.

    X2F never creates keys for you, because that would expose your private key. You need your PGP app at every login.

  • Every login message is signed with the X2F key, so your PGP app shows who sent it. The message also names the site and the time.

    [X2F SIGNING KEY FINGERPRINT]

    If the signature is missing or the fingerprint doesn't match, stop.

  • A passkey is a login stored on your phone or computer and unlocked with your face, fingerprint or device code. There is nothing to type and nothing to phish.

    Apple or Google may sync it between your devices. X2F learns nothing from that.

  • Use your recovery code. It starts a 24 h hold, and everyone on your account is logged out until it ends.

    X2F has no way to reach you, since it holds no email or phone number, so keep the code somewhere safe.

    Your coins are never at risk. They stay in your own wallet.

Wallets and privacy

  • Never. You pay from and get paid to wallets you already use.

    The app makes one temporary escrow key per trade on your device. It holds no balance and is deleted once the trade closes.

  • Cake Wallet, Edge, Feather, Monerujo, Stack, and the Monero GUI or CLI. A Ledger or Trezor works through Feather.

    Any wallet that gives you a Monero address can get paid.

  • No IP addresses, and no name, phone number or email address.

    Chats are deleted after 30 days and disputes after 90 days.

    During a trade X2F keeps an encrypted original copy of your payment proof that only a moderator can open, and only if there is a dispute. It is deleted when the trade is over.

  • Before upload, your phone strips the photo's metadata. Our privacy tool hides private details such as account numbers and names.

    You check the result and can add more blur. What our privacy tool hid stays hidden. The seller sees it with your private details hidden.

  • X2F answers a lawful request backed by a warrant with the little it holds.

    That is why X2F logs no IP addresses and asks for no name, phone number or email address.

Badges and reputation

  • Badges are milestones you earn by trading. They can't be bought or claimed.

    New means fewer than 5 completed trades. Trusted means 100 or more trades with 98% or more positive reviews.

    There are no volume badges, so nobody can see how much money you move.

  • If you traded on LocalMonero or a similar site, you can prove it with the PGP key you used there. A moderator checks an archived copy of your old profile against a message you sign now.

    It becomes a separate badge. It is never added to your X2F trade count or rating.
