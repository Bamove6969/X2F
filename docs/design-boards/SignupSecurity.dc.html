Decided · owner, 1 Nov, drawn 9 Nov (S-015) "How strong your login is" uses the owner's draft per tier, in plain words.
  • Weak: weak by industry standards; proving ownership after a break-in is slower; the 24 h cooling-off stops an intruder but applies to you too while we check.
  • Strong: stops simple guessing; decisive actions right away.
  • Top tier: "Armored vault!" (the owner's phrase).
  • Recovery note: keep the code apart from everything, with a light joke (a fake rock in the garden).
