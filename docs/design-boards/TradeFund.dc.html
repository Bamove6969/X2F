Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G2) Before the escrow is funded, either side can cancel at once. No reason, nothing to refund.
  • A "Cancel trade" text button sits under the wallet actions. One tap closes the trade: fund=cancelled shows the result, "You cancelled the trade" with "Nothing has been sent yet, so nothing needs refunding."
  • Buyer's view of this state (not drawn as its own screen): the same trade header with "Waiting for satoshi_ghost to fund the escrow" and the time-to-fund timer, "Don't send any money yet", and the same Cancel trade button with the same instant result. The other trader is told in the chat.
  • After funding, cancelling follows CancelRequest/SellerRefund (refund closes at 10 confirmations); opening a dispute before "I've paid" (p11-16) is drawn on SellerRefund.
