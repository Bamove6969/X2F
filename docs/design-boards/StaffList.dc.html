Design notes

Design · 12 Nov · D-032 (G60), D-070 (p11-19)Owner only: "Reset security" under each active member's actions.
  • A 44 px quiet text button with a key icon, under Switch off. It opens a danger confirm ("Reset dmitri_k's security?") that says what goes and what stays; "Tap key and reset" is one security-key tap (intent reset-staff-security). Then the reset link panel with Copy link; the row's status reads "Reset sent" (caution dot) until they finish.
  • The link opens StaffInvite with link=reset: never "You're invited as Moderator".
  • Invite lines follow D-070: moderators and seniors "They set up a PGP key and a passkey. A security key, such as a YubiKey, is optional."; helpers "They set up two keys: a security key or passkey and a PGP key, or two PGP keys." The old "a new device asks for two of their three" is dropped until the lead confirms the login rule without a key.
  • New text COPY-UNSIGNED. Preview with confirm=reset or reset-link. Showing: {{confirmName}}

Names and numbers are examples. Login hours are typed as 24-hour UTC times (HH:MM), matching the table.

  • Craft pass, 7 Nov: moderators and senior moderators are drawn with the --mod shield tile (32 in the table, 40 in the rail); the helper gets the neutral initials avatar; a pending invite gets the pending tile (solid 1px line border).
  • Role tags are the shared role tag (Vendor tag shape from Badges), in --mod with the shield for moderator roles, neutral for Helper. Role cards lead with a 40 icon tile.
  • Header: logo D (owner, 8 Nov, page 8); in the wide rail the page name sits under the logo so it never cuts off (comment #2).
