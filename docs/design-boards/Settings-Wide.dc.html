Design notes

Decided · owner, 2 Nov Owner facts this screen uses
  • Up to 3 devices logged in at once. One device at a time is optional and off by default.
  • Session length is chosen at every login: traders 2, 4, 6 or 12 h; vendors 2, 6, 12 or 24 h. The row shows the last choice.
  • The 24 h wait for new payout wallets is opt-in, not the default.
  • Team members are for vendor accounts only, up to 3. Switching to Vendor needs a passkey, security key or PGP key.
