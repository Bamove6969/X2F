1. What X2F is

X2F is a place where people buy and sell Monero (XMR) with each other. X2F is not a party to any trade. Coins in a trade sit in a 2-of-3 escrow, and X2F never holds them.

2. Your account

Every account has a username, and you keep your own login keys safe. X2F can't reset a password, because we don't know who you are. On an account with a username and password, important changes wait 24 hours before they apply. An account not signed into for 60 days is closed for security; an open trade or dispute pauses that clock. [Lawyer: eligibility, one person per account.]

3. Trades and escrow

Funding an escrow is a commitment, and a release can't be undone. "Pay now" only shows once the escrow is secured. Each trade shows its own time limits, which depend on the payment type, and only the rules that apply to you in that trade. Site maintenance pauses trade timers.

4. Fees

The person who posted the ad pays 1% when a trade completes. The person who takes an ad pays no X2F fee. A full reversal by a moderator has no fee. Network fees are separate. Extra XMR sent to an escrow before the buyer taps "I've paid", within the offer's limits, grows the trade to the funded amount, and both sides see the new total; otherwise the extra goes back to the seller. A refund returns everything to the seller.

5. Disputes, moderators and our role

X2F's role is to run the escrow and, when a trade goes wrong, to decide it fairly. Moderators can read a trade chat only if a dispute is opened. A moderator steps in within 24 hours at most. Coins move as soon as a ruling is made. Either side can appeal within 48 hours of a ruling. If a ruling is later reversed, a lien stays on the losing side until they pay back. Feedback and trade totals follow the final outcome. By signing, you say you understand these terms, the rules and our role.

6. Privacy and data

We don't log IP addresses. Completed trades, chat included, are erased for good on day 30 after they end. Disputed trades are kept for up to 90 days after the dispute closes, with private details removed by our privacy tool, and used to train our moderators while X2F grows. Your signature below is kept with your account; we don't record your IP address or device with it. No other copy is kept.

7. Police and government requests

If police or a government sends us a lawful request for data backed by a warrant, we will provide what little information we hold. That is exactly why we don't log IP addresses or ask for your name, phone number or email address: we can't hand over what we never collect. [Lawyer to word this.]

8. Prohibited use

Scams can lead to a lifetime ban. Trades in sanctioned currencies aren't allowed. [Lawyer: the full rules list.]

9. Liability, changes and law

When these terms change, you see the changes the next time you log in. [Lawyer: liability and governing law.]

  1. You're a moderator

    You settle disputes between traders. You rule on your own when a trade is under 1,000 USD; bigger rulings also need a senior moderator or the owner.

    Your login hours are Mon–Fri, 09:00–17:00 UTC.

  2. How you're paid

    You earn 4 USD in XMR for each case you complete. Your pay is worked out and sent at the end of your shift, when you clock out.

    Closing the app doesn't clock you out. Tap Clock out at the end of every shift. Until you do, your pay waits.

  3. Traders can appeal

    A trader who's unhappy with your ruling can appeal within 48 hours. A senior moderator or the owner looks at it again, never you.

    If your ruling is overturned, that case earns nothing: its pay comes off your next payouts. If you leave before then, you still owe it.
