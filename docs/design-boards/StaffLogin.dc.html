  1. Your role: Moderator

    You settle disputes between traders. You rule on your own when a trade is under 1,000 USD; bigger rulings also need a senior moderator or the owner.

    Your login hours: Mon–Fri, 09:00–17:00 UTC.

  2. How you're paid

    You earn 4 USD in XMR for each case you complete. Your pay is worked out and sent at the end of your shift, when you clock out.

    Closing the app doesn't clock you out. Tap Clock out at the end of every shift. Until you do, your pay waits.

  3. Traders can appeal

    A trader who's unhappy with your ruling can appeal within 48 hours. A senior moderator or the owner looks at it again, never you.

    If your ruling is overturned, that case earns nothing: its pay comes off your next payouts.
