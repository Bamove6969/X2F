Privacy

Every trade self-destructs

A trade self-destructs 30 days after it ends. Its chat, payment proofs and details are deleted for good, for both traders and for X2F.

If someone presses the dispute button, the trade self-destructs 90 days after that moment instead. That leaves time for the moderator's decision and any appeal.

You don't need to do anything, and there's nothing to choose. The date is shown at the top of every trade.

Moderator training

Moderators learn from past disputes. A dispute record is kept for 90 days and used to train moderators. Private details like names, bank details and wallet addresses are blurred automatically. After 90 days it's deleted. No other copy is kept.

What we never keep

  • IP addresses. We don't log them, not even during a dispute.
  • Who you are. No names, phone numbers or ID checks.
  • Hidden photo data. Your phone removes location and device details from proofs before they're sent.
