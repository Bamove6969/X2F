Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G23, G24, G25) The length is picked from a drop-down, with nothing pre-selected.
  • Traders: 2 h, 4 h, 8 h or 12 h
  • Vendors: 2 h, 6 h, 12 h or 24 h
  • A new choice starts counting from now.
  • Asked after logging in, not at signup: after signup you go to the login screen, log in, then pick it.
  • Header: logo D (owner, 8 Nov, page 8); in the wide rail the page name sits under the logo so it never cuts off (comment #2).

Decided · owner, 2 Nov Asked on every login, with nothing pre-selected. The times depend on the account type (current lists in the 7 Nov box above).
  • Session = the chosen time. At the end: confirm with a passkey, security key, PGP key or the password (plus authenticator code if set up) to extend by the same time; otherwise logged out (owner, 2 Nov). No fixed maximum.
