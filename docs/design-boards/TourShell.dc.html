Design notes

  • Owner, 12 Nov (comment 47 and brief item 2): a page-by-page tour, an arrow and a highlight per feature, a well-written card, Next, then Next screen. Same shell for the staff and team-member tours. Stops for every tour: docs/design-12nov/tour-stops.md.
  • X2F’s own look only (owner, 12 Nov): THEMES tokens, our type, our radii. Nothing copied from the Trust Wallet reference. The 3D scenes and the fades belong to T1’s motion board; this board is the still shell.
  • Behind: the BuyMain look for a trader account (3 tabs, no Post offer). The scrim is var(--scrim) with a cut-out hole, radius 14, 6 px round the element. The ring is 2 px ink, as for any selected thing. No shadow, no glow, no orange ring.
  • Arrow: one curved ink stroke, 2.5 px, round caps, open head, 40 px between the ring and the card. The arrow side is the card edge it leaves from. Orange stays on Next only.
  • Card: surface, 1 px line, radius 16, padding 20. Page bars (one per page, pill, 4 px) and a mono count for this page’s stops. Title Familjen 22/28, body 17/26: one step up from the app’s 16 (owner: fonts a bit bigger).
  • Controls: Skip tour (text), Back (secondary, off on stop 1 of page 1), Next (primary, 48 high). On a page’s last stop Next reads Next screen; on the last stop of the last page it reads Finish, then You’re all set.
  • Replay: Settings, Help group, Take the tour again (data-part tour-replay; ringed here only to point it out). The tour also starts from the landing screen’s Take the tour.
  • COPY-UNSIGNED: every card title and body, Skip tour, Next screen, Finish, You’re all set, Get started, Take the tour again, A quick walk through each screen, the tour note under Help, the Tour closed line, Restart.
