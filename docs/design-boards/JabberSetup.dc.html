Design notes

Showing: {{stepName}}

Opens from the Secure Jabber (XMPP) section on Alerts. Four steps with the step tweak: address, verify, events, done. Compact header as on Alerts; the field card and the callout are one card; the done step is the Results kit done_state over a Chat kit preview of the exact message. Turn off is a danger tertiary, never a primary.

Decided · owner, 9 Nov (p11-27, p11-31)A Jabber settings page where the user picks which events send an alert. OMEMO and Tor are mandatory.
  • New step events (tweak step events), between verify and done, and reached again from done ("Change which events") and from the Events row on Alerts.
  • SAMPLE events, all ticked by default: Trades: "A new trade starts", "The buyer marks the payment as sent", "XMR is released", "A dispute is opened or a moderator joins". Account: "A new login or a new device", "A team member logs in for the first time", "Login methods, wallets, team or settings change". The owner picks the final list on page 13 (p11-32); the "Sample list" label goes when he does.
  • No off switch for OMEMO or Tor (p11-31). Box: "Always OMEMO, always Tor. Every alert is end-to-end encrypted with OMEMO and sent over Tor. There's no switch for either: an alert sent in the clear could show someone that you trade on X2F."
  • Open for the lead: with chosen events, a message names the kind of event (sample "A new device logged in to your X2F account."), never amounts, names or wallets. p5-7 said messages name nothing; please confirm with the owner.
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (p5-7)An optional secure Jabber (XMPP) notification for account changes
  • Optional. Off until the user adds a Jabber address and proves it with a test message.
  • Every change (replaced 9 Nov by p11-27: the user picks the events; account changes are one of them, on by default).
  • Discreet. One short message per change: "An account change was made on your X2F account."
  • No details. Nothing in the message says what changed, and there is no link to it.
Why no details

A Jabber account can be read by others: the server that hosts it, anyone with the device, or anyone who gets the password. So the message never names the change, the wallet or the person. It only tells you to go and look; the change itself stays in X2F, behind your login. OMEMO and Tor are both required (owner, 9 Nov, p11-31): there is no off switch for either.
