Adding wallets later

This first wallet works right away. Any wallet you add later asks you to confirm it's you, and we always show an alert. Want extra protection? In Security settings, you can make newly added addresses wait 24 h before they can be paid.

Design notes

Decided · design, 12 Nov · research-wallet-connect · owner #31, #32No wallet names as buttons; scan or paste is the connect
  • No Monero wallet offers a MetaMask or WalletConnect-style connect (WalletConnect covers other chains only, and its relay would see users' IPs). So "connecting" means scanning the wallet's Receive QR or pasting its address: that works with every Monero wallet and involves no third party.
  • The wallet chips are gone. "Suggested wallets" is a plain list: no border, no pill, no chevron, not tappable. Never Exodus (dropped Monero Aug 2025), MyMonero (shut Jan 2026) or Trust Wallet (no Monero). Edge and Stack work by scan or paste but aren't suggested (no payment links; Edge gives no subaddress).
  • The camera is read on the device (getUserMedia + an on-device QR decoder); no frame leaves the phone. A monero: URI is accepted and stripped to its address; a monero-wallet: URI is refused and wiped at once (state invalid, reason keys).
  • Compare is required before saving, scan or paste: clipboard malware swaps addresses, and the outlined first and last groups are what changes.
  • The Welcome checklist item 3 shows the method: "Scanned your wallet's Receive QR" or "Pasted your wallet's address", with the address end (owner #34).
Tweaks: state (7), invalid (5 reasons), addr (subaddress | main). The camera frame is tappable in the prototype to fake a found code. All new text is COPY-UNSIGNED.
