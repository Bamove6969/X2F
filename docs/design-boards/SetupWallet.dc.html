Where should we send your XMR?

Choose the wallet you want to be paid to. X2F never holds your coins.

For best privacy, turn on Full Sync in Edge's Monero settings. Otherwise Edge's servers can see payments coming in.

This first wallet works right away. Any wallet you add later asks you to confirm it's you, and we always show an alert. Want extra protection? In Security settings, you can make newly added addresses wait 24 hours before they can be paid.

Monero addresses never expire, so reusing one will never make you miss a payout. For extra privacy, you can paste a fresh address from your wallet for each trade.
