Design notes

Decided · owner, 7 Nov (F1, F2, F8) F1: each band's price is a choice of above or below market, then a typed percent. It maxes out at 50%. F2: payment instructions are per payment type; if left blank, the other trader sees "Payment method chosen: <type>. Please post pertinent details in chat window." F8: up to 3 buying and 3 selling offers at a time; the builder shows how many are used.

Band price. Above market / Below market is a two-choice control (selected = 2px ink ring), then a typed percent with "%" inside the field. The +/− stepper is gone. Over 50% shows the error under the field and blocks posting (tweak margin = too-high). The line under it shows the price at today's rate. Payment-type price. Each payment type now uses the same control (7 Nov): Above market / Below market / Same as band, then a typed percent with "%" inside and "Up to 50%." under it. Same as band (no change) is picked by default and hides the percent field. The +/− stepper is gone here too. Over 50% or an empty percent blocks posting, like a band. Offer count. The meta line under the title counts the side picked in "I want to".

Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q28, Q24/Q25) Currencies: the six most-used first, in this order: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY. Then all the rest A to Z. Every entry has a flag.

Currency chips. The six chips are those six, each with a flag (JPY added). "More ▾" opens every other currency A to Z, each with a flag; the More list isn't drawn here. A currency with no market rate shows there greyed, can't be picked, and carries "No rate right now", as on OfferAd (design call, 6 Nov; this replaces "hidden" below). Flags: real flag images from a vetted flag set at build time (placeholder here).

Decided · owner, 2 Nov One offer covers up to 5 currencies. The vendor types the amount bands per currency; nothing is converted or rounded. Pricing is market ± % only; there are no fixed-price offers. Bands are whole units, in order, with no overlap and no gaps (200–400, then 401–600). A currency with no market price is hidden for that currency only. (Design proposal, 6 Nov: show it greyed instead; see above.) Buyers filter by currency and method first and see prices in that currency. Payment instructions are per payment type and, where they differ, per currency (bank transfer: USD account and routing, EUR IBAN/SEPA).
