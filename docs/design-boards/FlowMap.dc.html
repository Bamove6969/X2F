Flow map

Which screen leads to which, so the build team can wire a demo from the finished boards. Eight flows, one column each; down a column is the main path. The written version with routes and demo paths is docs/design-flow-map-2026-11-08.md.

1  Entry and shell

16 boards

Logged out: Landing, Log in, Create account, recovery, nothing else (owner, 8 Nov). Then session and the Trade tab.

2  Signup

15 boards

Terms, tick box and signature first, once (owner, 8 Nov). Then set up the account. Ends on Login (G25).

3  Buy flow

21 boards

4  Sell flow

15 boards

satoshi_ghost posts an offer; mira.k takes it. FlexOffer-Post is the decided form (Q4).

5  Settings and account

6  Help and notices

Help rail item opens FAQ. CantDoThat is one shared popup; Maintenance and Ban-Notice are events.

7  Staff app

8 boards

Separate site, own rail: Overview, Disputes, Staff, Performance, Training library, Activity log, Settings.

8  Fold and desktop

9 boards

The same screens at other widths (Fold-Notes): under 600 phone, 600 to 999 two panes, 1000 and up three panes or the rail.

Legend

A screen: the board file name, and under it the tweak (state) to show at that step.
Arrow: the move the primary button, link or event makes. Down the column is the main path; the elbow is a branch.
Spine: a hub (Main tabs, Settings rows, Fold-Notes) and the screens its rows open.
Red ring and strike-through: kept for reference, not wired (Warn-IvePaid, BlockQueued).
Chip tones state the tweak, tab or event to show build a route for it exists on main (App.tsx) draft draft copy, words not yet signed superseded do not build staff staff site, moderator and owner tools width the same screen at another width

Design notes

Every phone board with a -Wide twin is the same screen at 1000 px and up with the six-item rail (Trade, Post offer, Wallets, Profile, Settings, Help); the twins are not drawn as separate nodes. A board that belongs to two flows (Block, TeamChat, BecomeVendor, Refunded, Review, SelfDestruct, TradeFund, Main) is drawn in each. Landing is the landing page drawn on the LogoDirections board, not a board file of its own. Names, amounts and times on the boards are examples: buyer mira.k, seller satoshi_ghost, trade K7M2-9QXA-4FTR-8WPN, 1.250000 XMR for $412.50 via Zelle.

Owner rules the arrows follow: Pay now and I've paid only after the escrow is secured (Q12); the buyer may cancel until I've paid, then only a dispute (g67); 90 minutes before a dispute on instant methods (F9); logged out, a visitor reaches only Landing, Login, recovery (Login-Backup) and signup, which starts with the Terms, tick box and signature on SetupTerms; no arrow reaches the marketplace before Login and SessionLength (owner, 8 Nov, comment #9); after signup the person logs in and then picks a session length (G25); held changes wait 24 hours and are listed on one view (g65); FlexOffer-Post is the offer form (Q4).
