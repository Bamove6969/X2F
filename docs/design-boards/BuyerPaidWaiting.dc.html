Decided · owner, 6 Nov (F10)Nudge: once every 15 min, with the Ahooga horn
  • The partner can be nudged once every 15 minutes.
  • Sound: the real two-blast car "Ahooga" horn, 3.64 s: the first blast sped up at the same pitch, the second at its original speed, nothing stretched (Freesound 480002 by craigsmith, CC0; the owner's final pick, 7 Nov). Files on the comms branch: assets/sound/x2f-nudge-final.wav and x2f-nudge-final-soft.wav (night). Before launch, cut it from the original WAV.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov Dispute opens after a wait set by the payment type (owner F9, 7 Nov): instant types such as Zelle 90 min; same or next day 24 h; slow types 48 h to 4 days. The wait pauses during maintenance. Preview before and after with the window tweak.
