Geometry (units of a 780 cap)
LettersX and 2 are the Familjen Grotesk 700 outlines, drawn as paths. The F is redrawn: stem and top bar as the font, the middle arm cut to a short stub.
SpacingHand-set: the 2 tucks into the X’s open side (gap 30 of 780), the F closes up on the 2’s flat base (gap 64).
CoinDiameter 196 of 780 (a quarter of the height), centred on the arm, 30 past the stub. Its right edge stops 12 short of the top bar, so the F keeps its shape.
SizeAlways given as the logo’s height. X2F has no ascenders or descenders, so height = cap height. Width = 2.43 × height.
2 · Rules

Clear space and minimum size

The coin is the unit: the clear space is one coin wide, so the rule scales with the logo and anyone can measure it.

Clear space · night, 160 px
24 px tallSmallest full-colour logo on screen
32 px tallSmallest one-colour logo (the ring needs room)
Clear spaceOne coin (its diameter, a quarter of the logo’s height) on every side. Nothing inside it: no text, edge, icon or line. At 24 px that is 6 px; the header’s en dash sits 8 px away.
Minimum sizeFull colour 24 px tall on screen, one-colour 32 px, print 8 mm. Below 24 px use the favicon, the coin F alone.
ScalingUse the cut for the size (24 and 32 small, 33–79 medium, 80 and up display). Never scale the 24 px file up to a hero; never scale the display file down to a header.
BackgroundGround, surface or surface-raised in either theme. On orange or a photo use the reversed version.
3 · Versions

One-colour and reversed

Same geometry, same coin position and size; only the fill changes. Shown at 96 and 32 px.

One-colour · night
Ink only. The coin becomes a ring, so it still reads as a coin with no orange.
One-colour · day
Use where only one colour prints or embosses: stamps, receipts, engraving, a fax.
Reversed · on orange
All one tone (on-monero, near black). The coin is solid with its rim cut out; orange is the ground, so the coin can’t be.
Reversed · on a photo
The same drawing in night ink (near white) over busy images. Photo is a stand-in.

6 · Use

Do and don’t

One rule under all of them: the drawing is fixed. Pick a version and a cut; never edit the paths.

Do
Ink letters, one orange coin, the cut for the size.
Don’t recolour the letters
Letters are ink only, never a state tone.
Don’t stretch or squash
Scale by height only; width follows (2.43 × height).
Don’t move the coin
The coin ends the F’s middle arm. Nowhere else.
Orange only on the coin
An orange letter takes the one accent away from the coin and the primary action.

Design notes

  • Owner, 8 Nov (p8-1): logo D, Wordmark, X2F in Familjen Grotesk 700 with one Monero-orange coin on the F. Comment #2: “logo – page name” top left of every page; p8-2: browse is “Find a trade”.
  • Drawn, not typed: the X and 2 are Familjen 700 outlines turned into paths, re-spaced by hand; the F is redrawn so its middle arm is a stub that ends in the coin. The coin is 196 of 780 across (a quarter of the height) at every size; the small cut rounds it to 6 px at 24 and 8 px at 32.
  • Colours are tokens only: letters currentColor (ink), coin --monero, the display rim a 74% mix of --monero over --on-monero. Favicon and app icon files carry fixed night colours because they can’t read CSS.
  • Checked at actual size and zoomed (16 px favicon, 24 and 32 px headers, night and day): stems and bars on whole pixels, the coin round with clear space to the arm and the bar.
  • COPY-UNSIGNED: every sentence on this board (headings, rules, captions and these notes). Page names come from LogoDirections and are already listed there.
Anticipated · design, 8 Nov
  • The market rate with source and time in the wide header (“Market rate · updated 14:05 UTC”), carried from LogoDirections; follows owner comment #3.
  • A reversed version on a photo stand-in and a home-screen preview of the app icon with its “X2F” label, so the icon choice can be judged where it lives (follows p8-1).
