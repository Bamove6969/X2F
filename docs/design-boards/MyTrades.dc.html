How a trade goes

  1. 1The seller locks the XMR in escrow.
  2. 2The buyer pays the seller directly, by the method on the offer.
  3. 3The seller checks the money arrived and releases.
  4. 4The XMR goes to the buyer's wallet. If something goes wrong, a moderator decides.

Header: logo D (owner, 8 Nov, page 8) with the page name (comment #2). Anticipated · design, 8 Nov “My trades” is not in the LogoDirections page-name table; it repeats this screen’s title (follows comment #2).

Anticipated · design, 8 Nov · follows owner comment #4 (never make me go back to remember)My trades: every open and recent trade in one place
  • New board (prototype review P1: once a trade started there was no way back to it). The trade tab opens here; every trade's back arrow comes back here.
  • Active first, then Recent. Each card: key mark tile in the trade's state tone (caution: your move or theirs; danger: dispute; escrow: completed; ink-muted: cancelled), the partner with a 40 px avatar, amount right-aligned in mono (fiat over XMR), the state chip and the time left or the date.
  • Stat tiles: active, need you (your move), completed (cast: satoshi_ghost's 1,284). "Find offers" goes to the trade home; "My offers" sits at the end as the next stop.
  • Empty: the trade empty state (hero key mark in ink-muted), one primary "Find an offer", then "How a trade goes" (the stepper's four steps) and "Post an offer", so the empty screen isn't bare (#10). Preview with the list tweak.
  • Main (off-limits today) should link its Active trades heading here ("See all"): for Main's designer.
Decided · owner, 28 Oct (appeals; 13:34 window = 48 h) A decided dispute shows "File appeal" in Recent until its 48 hours are up, then the button goes.
