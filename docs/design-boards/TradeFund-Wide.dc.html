Design notes

Decided · design, 12 Nov · research-wallet-connectLead with the QR; "Open in your wallet app" is secondary
  • Every wallet reads the monero: QR; only Cake Wallet and Monerujo open the link (Edge, Stack and Feather don't register it). So the send card (QR + copy fields) comes right after the hero, and the wallet link is a secondary press-and-hold button in the footer, phones only (iOS, Android, touch Mac).
  • The label never names an app: the phone picks which wallet opens. "Open in Cake Wallet" is gone, and so is "Use another wallet" (the code and copy fields are always on screen).
  • QR and link carry monero:<escrow>?tx_amount=1.2625&tx_description=X2F%20trade%20K7M2; copy gives the same amount. After a short payment both carry only the rest (tweak pay=rest). The hold (1.5 s) and the "copy counts as starting to fund" rule stay as built.
  • No orange primary on the phone while funding: the work happens in the wallet. On wide, "Copy address" is the one primary (as built).
