Design notes

  • Same anatomy as the phone board. Showing: {{sideName}}.
  • Vendor. The trader block from the Badges board: 48px avatar (teal ring because this trader holds Trusted; online dot), name with a Vendor tag, "Online · replies in about N min", three stat tiles (trades, % positive, XMR traded), then up to 2 featured badges and "+N". The chevron opens the profile.
  • Badges (badges tweak, showing: {{badgesState}}). Pointing at "+N" (or Enter on it) opens a card under it: surface-raised, 1px line border, 12px radius, a nub, no shadow. It lists the badges behind "+N" with what each means and when it was earned, and stays open while the pointer is on the badges or the card. Featured badges are buttons too and open the same list. Badges are not status chips: sentence case, no wash fill; the colour lives in the seal.
  • Volume line (opt-in, volumeLine tweak). "{{volumeText}} XMR traded to date", shown as the third stat tile ("XMR traded"); with it off, that tile shows the star average and review count instead. The vendor turns it on; off by default. It counts XMR, not dollars (owner, 6 Nov, Q63). Rounded down to one decimal: it is a reputation figure, not an amount being moved, so the 6-decimal rule for amounts does not apply, and rounding down never overstates. Showing: {{volumeState}}{{volumeRaw}}.
  • Opens on the currency and payment method you searched for. Both drop-downs sit in the hero, next to the price they change, payment type right under currency.
  • Currency drop-down (currencyMenu tweak, showing: {{menuState}}). The six most-used currencies come first, always in this order: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY. Then a divider, then every other currency A to Z by code. An ad lists only the currencies its vendor ticked (up to 5), in that same order. Every row starts with a flag. The picked row has the 2px ink ring on the raised surface, never orange. On wide the open list is 360 wide, wider than the field, so the no-rate tag fits.
  • No market rate. ARS has no rate right now in this example. It stays in the list, greyed, and can't be picked (aria-disabled), with a "No rate right now" tag on the right. It comes back the moment a rate returns. Why show it rather than hide it: people can see their currency exists and will come back for it; hiding it looks like X2F dropped it.
  • Flags: real flag images from a vetted flag set at build time (placeholder here).
  • {{ad.methodLabel}} drop-down (payMenu tweak, showing: {{payState}}). Right under Currency: pick the currency first, then the payment type. The closed field shows the chosen type and its time to pay ("Zelle · 90 min to pay"). Each row: the type, its time to pay (plus its own limit, if it has one) and the price difference in mono ("+1.5%"); same panel as the currency list, selection is the 2px ink border, never orange.
  • "All ways to pay" list removed (7 Nov). Every fact it held (time, limit, price difference) is now in the drop-down rows; the price for the chosen type is the hero number. So the drop-down is the one way to choose.
  • Currencies and payment types on an ad are indexed, so buyer searches find them (owner F7).
  • The trade panel is the right column, so the amount and the button stay next to the price. The secondary is a full outline button here; on the phone it's a tertiary link to keep the pinned footer short.
  • Button: {{ad.cta}}. The vendor still has to accept.
  • Escrow row: neutral 40px shield tile. The 2-of-3 key mark stays reserved for the three places in system v2 §1.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov 2026
  • One ad covers every currency the vendor supports. The trader picks currency and payment method from the drop-downs; both are locked in for that trade. Payment-method rules (time to pay, name match…) come with the method.
  • Ad limit: 3 buy + 3 sell ads per vendor.
  • Each currency has its own bands set by the vendor; nothing is converted. Up to 5 currencies per ad. Limits and tier prices shown are the chosen currency's own figures. A currency the vendor ticked but that has no market price is hidden from the Currency drop-down for that currency only. Design proposal, 6 Nov: show it greyed instead (see the notes above).
  • Volume line is opt-in. Never shown unless the vendor turns it on. Wording and unit replaced 6 Nov (Q63), below.
Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q63, Q28, Q24/Q25)
  • Q63. The reputation line shows the amount in XMR traded to date, not dollars: "275.4 XMR traded to date".

  • Q28, Q24/Q25. Currency drop-down: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY first, a divider, then all the rest A to Z. Every entry has a flag.
