Design notes

Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q28, Q24/Q25) Currencies: the six most-used first, in this order: USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, JPY. Then all the rest A to Z. Every entry has a flag.

Currency chips. The six chips are those six, each with a flag (JPY added). "More ▾" opens every other currency A to Z, each with a flag; the More list isn't drawn here. A currency with no market rate shows there greyed, can't be picked, and carries "No rate right now", as on OfferAd (design call, 6 Nov). Flags: real flag images from a vetted flag set at build time (placeholder here).

Open · owner to choose Two "Post an offer" boards are live. Which one ships?
  • This board: one price (market ± %) and one size range per currency.
  • FlexOffer-Post: up to 5 price bands per currency, each with its own ± %.

Prices and ranges are examples. Helper copy on this board is COPY-UNSIGNED.

Craft pass, 7 Nov: the form reads as a guided builder. Each card opens with a step head (40 px icon tile, "Step N of 4", heading). Preview label gets an eye icon. COPY-UNSIGNED: "Step N of 4", "What you trade", "Trade size and terms".

8 Nov, owner #11 (offer unit). Each range box has an XMR / currency switch inside its right end (unit tweak, showing: {{unitState}}; default XMR, the left one). Selected unit: 15/20 600 in --monero-ink; the other 12/16 ink-muted. One unit per offer: tapping either switch moves every box (min and max, every currency) together (lead default; the owner may override). Examples: XMR 0.500000 – 25.000000; USD $500 – $3,000, EUR €500 – €2,800. Under each box the other unit, converted at the poster's own price (market ± their margin), so the margin stepper changes it. A fiat range still sells XMR. Boxes stack one per row: the switch needs the width. The preview shows what traders see: markup bottom-left, the range in XMR and in each currency right-aligned in mono (converted rows start with "≈").

8 Nov, owner #12. "Minimum completed trades" is five fixed chips: Anyone (kept first, the default, lead default "No minimum"), 5, 10, 25, 50; the picked chip has the 2 px ink ring. Replaces the drop-down (which had 5+ / 10+ / 50+, no 25).
