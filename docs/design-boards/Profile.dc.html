Design notes

Draft copy

Trade volume line. An opt-in row under the stats, escrow tone with a bar-chart glyph (no trophy): "41.7 XMR traded to date". Rounded down to one decimal: it is a reputation figure, not an amount being moved, so amounts' 6-decimal rule does not apply, and rounding down never overstates. The same line goes on the trader's ads. The trader turns it on in Settings › Profile visibility (ProfileVisibility board). The volumeLine tweak previews it on or off; it is on here.

Decided · owner, 2 Nov 2026
  • Opt-in. Off until the trader switches it on. Never shown without their choice.
  • X adds buying and selling. The dollar steps that were here are replaced by XMR (6 Nov, Q63), below.
Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q63, Q64)
  • Q63. The line shows the amount in XMR traded to date, not dollars. Example here: 41.738201 XMR shows as "41.7 XMR traded to date".
  • Q64. Volume imported from other sites is one line per site ("Imported from LocalMonero: 120.0 XMR"). This profile shows no imported volume, so no such line is drawn.
Decided · owner, 1 Nov (item 27), 28 Oct
  • Item 27. After the trade self-destructs, its review shows month and year only ("Sep 2026").
  • 28 Oct. A review its trader disputes shows "Under review" at once, until a moderator decides (review tweak). Hiding its stars and text meanwhile is a design reading.
  • COPY-UNSIGNED: "Under review", "A moderator is checking this review."

Badges. The seal list from the Badges board: a 40 px seal per badge (family tone: reputation teal, speed amber, security and milestones ink), its name, what it means and when it was earned. The header avatar uses the same 48 px treatment as offers: teal ring: mira.k holds Trusted (212 trades · 99%, rule 200+), teal online dot. Badge meanings and dates are COPY-UNSIGNED (draft).
