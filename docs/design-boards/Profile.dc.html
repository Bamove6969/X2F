Design notes

Draft copy

Trade volume line. An opt-in row under the stats, escrow tone with a bar-chart glyph (no trophy): "335.6 XMR traded to date". Rounded down to one decimal: it is a reputation figure, not an amount being moved, so amounts' 6-decimal rule does not apply, and rounding down never overstates. The same line goes on the trader's ads. The trader turns it on in Settings › Profile visibility (ProfileVisibility board). The volumeLine tweak previews it on or off; it is on here.

Decided · owner, 2 Nov 2026
  • Opt-in. Off until the trader switches it on. Never shown without their choice.
  • X adds buying and selling. The dollar steps that were here are replaced by XMR (6 Nov, Q63), below.
Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q63, Q64)
  • Q63. The line shows the amount in XMR traded to date, not dollars. Example here: 335.627411 XMR shows as "335.6 XMR traded to date".
  • Q64. Volume imported from other sites is one line per site ("Imported from LocalMonero: 120.0 XMR"). This profile shows no imported volume, so no such line is drawn.

Names, stats, review dates and reviews are examples.
