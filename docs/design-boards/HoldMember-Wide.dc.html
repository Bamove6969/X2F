Design notes

Showing: team member desk_ana · rights: {{rightsName}}

Decided · owner, 7 Nov (p4-8, p5-7) While the lead's changes wait 24 h, a team member sees only what their "Holds and waiting changes" rights allow. The default is none: they see that a hold is on, not what is waiting or who made it.
  • Tweak rights: none (default) shows the caution hero, the member's own row and a muted block saying what they can see. No list, no buttons except Back.
  • see ("See waiting changes"): the same hero, then HoldChange's approve-step list read-only, no ticks; each row reads before → after → "Applies in 23 h 12 min".
  • cancel ("Untick or cancel waiting changes"): the ticks come back and Cancel all changes (danger tertiary) with HoldChange's cancel confirm. No primary: approving is the lead's.
  • end ("End a hold"): as cancel plus the primary End the hold (48 px on wide, 56 px on phone). It needs a strong login, so it opens the Confirm it's you sheet; every ticked change then applies at once.
  • The list is copied from HoldChange (markup, data and effect lines). One swap: the removed member is desk_sam, not desk_ana, because desk_ana is the member signed in here.
  • The member's row uses the team kit: --team avatar with initials in --ground, the Team member tag, "In 4 of 5 types · 10 of 33 allowed". The hero is v2 §6 in caution; the result states (hold ended, changes cancelled) use the shared done_state.
Decided · owner, 8 Nov (p7-9, p7-12, p10-2) Members may log in any way, but a username-and-password lead still puts team changes on the 24 h hold, even when a strong member makes them.
