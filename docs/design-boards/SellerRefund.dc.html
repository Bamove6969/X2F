Decided · owner, 8 Nov (time to pay expired, trade still open)The hero says the time to pay has expired but the trade has not ended, and lists the three options.
  • Chip, headline, body and the three rows are design's wording, asked for by the owner. Same hero on available and late.
  • The buttons stay: Cancel primary, Nudge secondary. The line under them ("A nudge often brings a quiet buyer back…") is dropped: the third row says it.
Decided · owner, 8 Nov (p6-1)Nudges are optional, never a gate.
  • The 8 Nov reversal removes the nudge gate: no "cancel: locked" step any more. After the time to pay, Cancel and get your XMR back is the primary at once.
  • Nudge mira.k stays as the secondary, at most once every 15 min, and is encouraged in the hero's list of options (8 Nov).
  • Removed: "Give 30 more min". The trade stays open until you cancel (p9-2), so waiting needs no button.
Decided · owner, 8 Nov (p9-2)The time to pay is not a hard end.
  • No 15-minute final warning to mira.k. Cancelling starts the refund at once; the trade is done at 10 confirmations (cancel: done).
  • Until you cancel, mira.k can still message and pay (cancel: late shows "I just got back, sending now").
  • If mira.k paid late and you cancel, they can open a dispute at once ("If mira.k paid after all").
Decided · owner, 8 Nov (p10-1)You escrow the trade plus 1%.
  • The deal summary adds "You escrow 1.262500 XMR: 1.250000 for the trade + 0.012500 X2F fee (1%)". Only the selling poster sees it; mira.k gets the full 1.250000.
  • A cancel returns all 1.262500 XMR: no fee on a full reversal (g63). The lead's brief said "get your 1.250000 XMR back"; design used 1.262500 to match p10-1. Lead to confirm.

Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G2) Who can cancel, and when
  • Before escrow is funded: either side can cancel at once, no reason needed.
  • Seller, after escrow: once the time to pay has ended with no I've paid, Cancel is there at once and the escrowed XMR goes back to the seller's wallet. No nudge is needed (p6-1, 8 Nov, replaces the 7 Nov nudge rule).
  • Buyer, after escrow: can cancel. That triggers a full refund to the seller, and the trade stays open until the refund has all 10 confirmations.
