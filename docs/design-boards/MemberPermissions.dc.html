Types. Each type is a card with a disclosure header (64px): name, count, and a switch on the right. Switch on = desk_ana is in that type; the card opens to its checkboxes (44px rows, 22px boxes, ink accent; a row with a hint grows to fit it). Switch off = the card closes and greys out. Its ticks are kept but disabled, and don't count, until the type is switched back on. Count. The summary counts only the ticks in types that are on. Tweak example: new-member (default), manager, offers-and-chat, everything, trades-on-schedule.
