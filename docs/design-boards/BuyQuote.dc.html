Design notes

Craft pass, 7 Nov: the seller is drawn as a person (compact trader block from the Badges kit, same data as BuyOffers); "You receive" is the screen's hero (v2 §6 neutral wash, no key mark before a trade exists); wallet rows get 40 px wallet tiles.

8 Nov, owner #4 and #11 (bands on the quote). Right under the amount and rate: "This offer's bands", so the buyer never goes back a screen to see where the price clips. Each band: its range in the offer's unit (USD, set by the vendor) and in XMR ("≈"), its rate and markup; the band holding your amount has the 2 px ink ring and a "Your amount" tag. Under the list, where it clips: "Above $500 the rate changes to $331.88." (and the reverse once you are in band 2). "With markup / Without" (bandsView tweak, showing: {{obViewState}}; default with) converts the XMR column at each band's price, or at the market rate ($329.90) with the freshness line. Example bands for satoshi_ghost: $50 – $500 at $330.00 (the cast trade, $412.50 → 1.250000 XMR) and $500.01 – $1,500 at $331.88; the brief's sample sentence said "Above $1,500", but this offer's largest trade is $1,500, so the clip sits at $500. The Rate row, the header line and You receive follow the band.

Max (owner #5, "I do really like the max button"). Kept from the prototype: a Max text button inside the You pay field fills the offer's largest trade ($1,500); the bands card then moves the ring to band 2.
