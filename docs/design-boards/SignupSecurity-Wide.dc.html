What an authenticator app can't do on its own

It's fine for logging in with your password. But some sensitive actions need a passkey, security key or PGP key instead:

  • Ending a 24 h hold early
  • Logging in on a new device when your other devices aren't with you
  • Adding team members, changing their roles or switching them on and off
  • Switching to a vendor account

Add one of those too and you won't be limited.
