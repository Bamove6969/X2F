Decided · owner, 2 Nov Only a recovery-code login logs everyone out of the account (it starts the 24-hour security hold). Its open trades are then checked.
  • Not funded: the trade is cancelled.
  • Funded: the trade is paused while X2F contacts the held account holder.
  • A submitted username and password change does not log anyone out; it just waits 24 h.
  • So these partner messages appear only after a recovery-code login.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov The trade partner gets short, calm, non-technical messages ("make it not a word salad").
  • Never say "hold", "security" or "vendor" to the partner.
  • Say the other trader's account "has a temporary problem".
Case tweak (partner side)
  • unfunded: nothing in escrow, trade cancelled.
  • funded-you-sold: the partner funded escrow and waits for the held account to pay.
  • funded-you-bought: the held account funded escrow; the partner pays.
  • resolved-refund: the held account hadn't paid, so the partner's XMR goes back.
  • resolved-resume: the held account is back and the trade continues.
The side tweak shows the held account's own view (copy unchanged); the case tweak only changes the partner view.
