Design notes

Owner, 12 Nov (/sell comment)The first screen after the first login: Welcome to X2F, then Take the tour or Skip and get started.
  • “Welcome to” runs on a 20° diagonal from the top left down to the big logo in the middle (logo D, display cut, 240 px wide on phone, 292 on wide).
  • The tab bar (phone) and the rail (wide) show as if they had already dived in. No tab is current: this screen is none of them.
  • FAQ, Getting started, Support and Settings fill the space under the two choices.
  • Take the tour opens the tour (TourShell). Skip and get started goes to returnTo if setup started from a take or a post, else Find a trade (/sell). Either choice marks the screen seen.
Decided · design, 12 NovWhen it shows: once per account, on the first full login after signup.
  • Order: signup ends on /setup/welcome, Continue to log in ends the signup session, /login, /session-length, then this screen (/welcome) before returnTo or /sell.
  • It is behind the front door (nothing visible before login): logged-out visitors still get Landing (Create account, Log in, recovery). Landing is not replaced.
  • After a choice it does not come back on later logins; Getting started (Help) and Take the tour again (Settings) reopen the tour. A member of a team never sees it (their lead’s account is set up).
Header ruleException to logo top-left with the page name: like Landing, the big centred logo is this screen’s logo (the owner drew it in the middle).
  • Browser tab title: “Welcome · X2F”. Wide keeps the rail lockup (logo, then “Welcome” under it).
Motion (build)Entry, after the intro slot starts.
  • “Welcome to” slides in along its own diagonal: from -24, -9 px and opacity 0, 420 ms cubic-bezier(.2,.8,.2,1), delay 150 ms.
  • Logo: opacity 0 and scale 0.96 to 1, 360 ms ease-out, delay 350 ms. Buttons and links: up 8 px and fade, 240 ms, delay 600 ms.
  • Reduced motion: everything is there at once, the slot shows its still frame. Nothing loops.
Intro slotReserved for the isometric intro (another worker builds the scene).
  • Phone: full width minus the 16 px gutters, 300 px tall; on short phones it shrinks to clamp(240 px, 100dvh − 544 px, 300 px) so both choices stay above the tab bar (the owner’s 443 × 794 gets 250).
  • The blocks drawn here are a placeholder still in token colours, not the final art. role="img", aria-label “X2F intro animation”.
