Going back to trader

{{backText}}

Switch to a vendor account?

  • You can post offers and add team members.
  • You pay the 1% fee on completed trades.
  • Session length: 2, 6, 12 or 24 h.
  • You can't switch back. To be a trader again, open a new account.

Design notes

Decided · owner, 2 Nov Team members are only for vendor accounts Owner: "team members are only part of vendors". A trader account has no team.
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G26) A passkey, security key or PGP key is the only requirement. Any trader can apply, even a beginner. No minimum number of trades.
OPEN · owner to choose Does using a recovery code, or logging in with just a username and password, remove vendor status?
  • No: vendor status stays
  • Paused: offers are hidden until the next login with a passkey, security key or PGP key
  • Yes: the account goes back to trader
Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q77), 7 Nov (G14) A vendor can't switch back. To trade as a trader again, they open a new account. The Switch back button and its sheet are gone. G14: a new account can reuse the person's passkey, security key or PGP key, but needs a new username and Trade Tag. COPY-UNSIGNED: the Going back to trader text and the new line in the switch sheet.
