Design notes

Decided · owner, 1 Nov (S-014)A live meter in words. Only a strong password is accepted.
  • Three bars under the password, then "Strength: …". Weak (1 bar, danger), Medium (2 bars, ink-muted), Strong (3 bars, escrow).
  • meter: checking: every rule is met but the server has not checked the leaked list yet. "Strength: Meets the rules", three neutral bars, no colour, and the leaked-list line is neither ticked nor crossed. "Set password" stays on: the server's answer is the check.
  • A leaked password drops to Weak and the leaked-list line turns to a cross.
  • The same meter state applies on SignupSecurity.
