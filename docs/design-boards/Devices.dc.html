Three ways in, from the usual one to the last resort.

  1. 47 Usual way

    Approve it on a logged-in device

    Your other devices ask "Is this you?". Tap the 2-digit number the new device shows. Say no and the login is blocked.

Decided · owner, 6 Nov (F18)Session length here changes this session only
  • Picking a new length on Devices changes how long this login lasts. The next login asks again, with nothing pre-selected.
  • A new choice starts counting from now (G24).
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G57) With a passkey, security key or PGP key, switching a member off or on applies at once. With just a username and password, switching off logs the member out at once and is final after 24 h (row: "Logged out · final in 24 h"); switching back on waits 24 h. COPY-UNSIGNED. Preview with the session tweak. Showing: {{sessionName}}
Decided · owner, 6 Nov (Q75) · 7 Nov (G34, G32) Idle reminder: off by default; when on, the person picks the interval (draft 15 / 30 / 60 min). Traders can also get in on a new device with password + authenticator code; vendors still need a passkey, security key or PGP key. The recovery code only lets you set up login methods again. COPY-UNSIGNED. Preview with the idleReminder and accountType tweaks.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov "Log out all other devices" covers only your own devices. Use "Log out all team members" for your team.
Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G23) No default: the drop-down starts empty, for both account types. Preview a picked value with the idleDefault tweak. Showing: {{idleDefaultName}}
Decided · owner, 2 Nov · 7 Nov (G23) Session length choices depend on the account type
  • Traders: 2 h, 4 h, 8 h or 12 h
  • Vendors: 2 h, 6 h, 12 h or 24 h
  • Session = the chosen time. At the end: confirm with a passkey, security key, PGP key or the password (plus authenticator code if set up) to extend by the same time; otherwise logged out (owner, 2 Nov). No fixed maximum.

Decided · owner, 2 Nov The password also works for extending, plus the authenticator code when the account has one. Accounts with only a username and password can extend too.
Decided · owner, 2 Nov A vendor is an account switched to Vendor in Settings > Account type. Switching needs a two-factor method you've already set up: a passkey, security key or PGP key. See BecomeVendor.
