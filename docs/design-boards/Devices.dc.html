Extra safety

One device at a time When you approve a new device, the one you were using is logged out. Only one device is ever logged in.

How a new device gets in

When you log in somewhere new, every device here asks "Is this you?" and you tap the number the new device shows. If you say no, the login is blocked.

Lost your only device? Log in with a backup.

X2F doesn't log IP addresses, so devices are named only by browser and system.
