How a new device gets in

  1. 47 Approve it on a logged-in device Your devices ask "Is this you?". Tap the 2-digit number the new device shows. If you say no, the login is blocked.
  2. No other device with you? Log in with a passkey, security key or PGP key. Log in another way
  3. Only have a password? Use your recovery code. That starts a hold of 24 hours before you get in. How the hold works

X2F doesn't log IP addresses, so devices are named only by browser and system.
