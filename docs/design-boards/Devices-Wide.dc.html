How a new device gets in

Three ways in, from the usual one to the last resort.

  1. 47 Usual way

    Approve it on a logged-in device

    Your other devices ask "Is this you?". Tap the 2-digit number the new device shows. Say no and the login is blocked.

    See the approval prompt

  2. No other device with you?

    Log in with a passkey, security key or PGP key.

    Log in another way
  3. Last resort

    Only have your username and password?

    Use your recovery code. It starts a 24 h hold, and everyone on your account is logged out until it ends.

    How the hold works

X2F doesn't log IP addresses, so devices are named only by browser and system.
