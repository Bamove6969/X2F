Decided · owner, 7 Nov (G50–G53, p4-3) Gift card rules
  • The person holding the escrow has 45 min to check the card once its details are handed over.
  • Photo or video proof; video is encouraged (log in, show the details, then send).
  • Cards bought within the last 30 days.
  • Full written value only; range cards (e.g. Visa $200–$500) may be partial and always need the video.
  • "Other" is kept as a brand choice.
