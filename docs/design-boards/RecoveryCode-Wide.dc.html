No 0, O, 1 or I, so it's hard to misread.

Keep it apart from everything else

  • On paper, inside a fake rock by the door.
  • Buried in the back yard. Draw yourself a map.
  • In separate online storage that holds nothing else of yours.

Not next to your password, not on the phone you log in with, and not with your PGP key. If one of those is stolen, this shouldn't go with it.

Using it starts a 24 h hold and logs everyone else out. You stay in only to set up your login methods again.

Type your recovery code from where you wrote it down. Dashes, spaces and capitals don't matter.
