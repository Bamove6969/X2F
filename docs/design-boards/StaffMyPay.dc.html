Design notes

Names, amounts and times are examples. The pay rate is the owner's (StaffPaySetup); figures here are illustrative.

Design · 12 Nov · D-041, D-045, Plan 4 Task 33My pay: what is unpaid, payouts, shifts and payout addresses (GET /staff/v1/pay)
  • Rail item "My pay" for paid staff (helpers, moderators, seniors); the owner has none. The rail foot keeps Clock out, the shift start and the reminder, on every staff page.
  • Not paid yet: one row per pay item since the last paid clock-out; a clawback row is danger-toned with a minus. Payouts: status chip "Waiting to send" (caution; the payroll wallet isn't wired yet) or "Sent" (escrow). Shifts: "Clocked out, paid", "Logged out, not paid" or "Logged out automatically. Not a clock-out: that pay waited."
  • Payout addresses: up to 5, shortened 4…4 in mono; adding one needs a passkey or security-key approval.
Design · 12 Nov · the clock-out sheetClock out: total, thank-you, address. Preview with the sheet tweak. Showing: {{sheetName}}
  • Centred dialog 520 wide over the scrim, radius 16, padding 28. Title "Clock out" Familjen 24/30. It opens from Clock out (rail foot or the Not paid yet card); Esc and the X close it.
  • pay: the item lines (tick in escrow, a take-back in danger), the total in mono 32 with the dollars at today's price, the owner's thank-you in Familjen 20/26, then "Send it to": the newest approved address is chosen (2 px ink border, filled dot). "Another address" opens the add step. Primary "Send my pay and clock out", quiet "Not yet".
  • owed: no address; caution box "Nothing is paid this time" with the rest that comes off next pay; primary "Clock out". no-price: caution box "Your pay waits for your next clock-out"; primary "Clock out".
  • add: paste a Monero address, approve with a passkey or security key, then back to the total with the new one chosen.
  • Motion: scrim fades 150 ms; the dialog rises 8 px and fades in over 200 ms, ease-out; reduced motion: no rise.
  • All text here is COPY-UNSIGNED. It tightens the build's ClockOut.tsx: "Thank you: we couldn't do it without you." becomes two plain sentences; "decisions overruled" becomes "rulings overturned"; the build's "Logging out or closing the app does not pay." keeps its meaning.
