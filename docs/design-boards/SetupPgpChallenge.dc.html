Check the signature before you paste

Your PGP app should show a good signature from the official X2F key, and the message should name X2F and today's date and time. If not, stop. Someone may be trying to phish you.
Key verified

Every login works this way from now on. X2F shows a message locked to your key, your PGP app unlocks it with your private key, and you paste in the code.
