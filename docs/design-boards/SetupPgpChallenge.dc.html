  1. Copy the encrypted message below.
  2. Open your PGP app (Kleopatra, GPG Suite or OpenKeychain) and decrypt it.
  3. Paste the code from inside the message.

The message is also signed with the official X2F PGP key, which proves it really came from us; your PGP app shows whether the signature is good. The decrypted message also names X2F and today's date and time. If the signature is missing or bad, or it names a different site or an old time, stop and don't paste anything. Someone may be trying to phish you.
