How it's sent

Every alert is end-to-end encrypted with OMEMO and sent over Tor. Apps without OMEMO won't work.

  1. 1Install a Jabber app with OMEMO: Conversations (Android), Monal (iPhone) or Gajim (computer).
  2. 2Connect the app through Tor, for example with Orbot.
  3. 3Type your address in the box and tap Send a test message.
  4. 4Accept the message from X2F. Trust its OMEMO key only if the fingerprint matches the one below.

Why only the kind of event

A Jabber account can be read by others: the server that hosts it, anyone with the device, or anyone who gets the password. So the message names only the kind of event ("A new device logged in to your X2F account."), never an amount, a name, a wallet or a trade code. It tells you to go and look; the details stay in X2F, behind your login. OMEMO and Tor are both required (owner, 9 Nov, p11-31): there is no off switch for either.
