Decided · owner, 7 Nov (p5-7)An optional secure Jabber (XMPP) notification for account changes
  • Optional. Off until the user adds a Jabber address and proves it with a test message.
  • Every change. Login methods, team, wallets or cash-out, settings and holds each send one message.
  • Discreet. One short message per change: "An account change was made on your X2F account."
  • No details. Nothing in the message says what changed, and there is no link to it.
Why no details

A Jabber account can be read by others: the server that hosts it, anyone with the device, or anyone who gets the password. So the message never names the change, the wallet or the person. It only tells you to go and look; the change itself stays in X2F, behind your login. OMEMO is recommended, not required, because the message gives nothing away even in the clear.
