Settings

Only the owner can change these. Every change is recorded in the activity log.

Rulings

USD equivalent A moderator can rule alone on trades below this. At or above it, a senior moderator or you must approve too.
hours How soon a moderator must reply after a dispute opens. Traders see this deadline and their place in the queue.
hours How long either trader has to appeal a ruling. Appeals go to a senior moderator or you, never the moderator who ruled.

Staff pay

Paid in XMR at the end of each shift, when the staff member logs out. Amounts to be decided; check with the lawyer before the first payment.
USD in XMR Includes co-approving large rulings.
% of the trade total Paid on top of the per-case rate for trades at or above the large-trade limit ($1,000). Example: 0.5% of a $4,000 trade is $20. USD in XMR
USD in XMR A ruling overturned on appeal earns nothing.
USD in XMR Flat rate, since questions don't come in cases.

Payout and clawback

When a staff member logs out, X2F adds up the shift and sends it to their payout wallet. They see the total before they confirm logging out.

If an appeal later overturns one of their rulings, the pay for that case is taken back from their next payout.
