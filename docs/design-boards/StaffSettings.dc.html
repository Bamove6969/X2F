Payout and clawback

When a staff member clocks out, X2F adds up every case they completed since they last clocked out. They see the total and a thank-you, confirm their payout address or pick another approved one, and the pay is sent right away. Closing the app is not a clock-out: their pay waits until they clock out, and a session that ends on its own is only noted in the activity log ("Logged out automatically at …").

If an appeal later overturns one of their rulings, they lose the pay for that case, and its large-trade bonus. Nothing is held back: the amount comes off their next payouts, and if they leave first it is recorded as owed.

Fee wallet

The fee wallet stays online to pay staff. When it reaches your threshold, everything above it moves to your cold wallet on its own. Only the cold wallet's address is kept here, never its keys.
