Design notes

Wide form of Lien-Notice: the hero and primary on the left, what it means and the appeal decision on the right. The appeal decision card ends with the fee row, inside the same inner block as the trade, decided-by and date rows.

Decided · owner, 7 Nov (g63)The 1% fee after a disputeA full reversal, where all funds go back to the original parties, carries no 1% fee: X2F pays the moderator itself. A ruling that moves the trade forward, an appeal that upholds completion, or a lien means the trade completes and the offer poster pays the 1%. While an appeal is open, the fee waits for the outcome. A lien is a completed trade, so here the poster, who is reading this notice, pays.
