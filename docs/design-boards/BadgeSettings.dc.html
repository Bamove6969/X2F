Design notes

Proposal · design, 7 Nov (owner liked tap/hover) Design's proposal follows the owner's 7 Nov comment that they like showing more on tap or hover. The middle option is renamed from "On hover" to "When tapped": those badges sit behind "+N" on offers. On a phone, tapping "+N" or a badge opens a sheet; on a computer, pointing at "+N" opens a small card (see OfferAd and OfferAd-Wide). Not decided yet: the owner confirms the name.
