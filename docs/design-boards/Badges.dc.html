Design notes

  • Owner, 7 Nov: likes badges showing more on tap or hover (device dependent); asked design to make the card and the badges look like part of the site. This board is design's answer.
  • Seal: 22 px on a card chip, 28 px on profile, 40 px in lists and the sheet. Family tone at 16% over surface-raised, a 55% tone ring, glyph in tone. Milestones use the number itself as the glyph, in mono.
  • Chip: 30 px, surface-raised, 1px border in the family tone at 32% (60% on hover or focus), name in ink 13/16 600, sentence case. Badges are not status chips: no uppercase, no wash fill.
  • Avatar ring turns teal only when the trader holds Trusted; the online dot is always teal. Stats: three raised tiles, mono values (trades, % positive, XMR traded to date).
  • The middle visibility option (G38) is the "+N": phone tap opens a sheet, desktop hover opens a tooltip card. Featured badges also open the same sheet or card when tapped.
  • Prices are examples at a $329.90 / XMR market. Badge names, meanings and dates are examples and COPY-UNSIGNED. Next: apply to OfferAd, BuyOffers/SellOffers, Profile and BadgeSettings once the owner likes this.
