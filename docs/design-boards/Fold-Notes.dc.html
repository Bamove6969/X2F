Design notes for the Fold boards

On Fold-Cover, Fold-Offers, Fold-MyOffers, Fold-Trade, Fold-Tabletop and Fold-TriFold the sellers, prices, amounts, times and the Zelle contact are examples. The Zelle contact shows masked, as the buyer would see it.

Open · owner to choose What does the buyer see in the "Zelle to" row (Fold-Trade, Fold-Tabletop, Desktop)?
  • The seller's full Zelle contact (phone number) as they entered it
  • A masked contact until the buyer taps to reveal it (shown on the boards)
  • Whether the seller can set a display name instead of a number
