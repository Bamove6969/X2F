## G3
Must every moderator hold all three: security key, PGP key and passkey?

Answer: All three are required. Two of them are required to log in on a new device, and one of them is required to log in on their existing device.

## G4
Should permanently removing a staff member, or an owner key, need a security key tap?

Answer: All security key methods require a tap. And obviously only an owner can be doing anything to do with an owner account, and that will require him producing both security keys if he's going to be making any major changes like changing one of his keys or something like that.

## G5
Should removing a wallet or team member also wait 24 hours when done with only a username and password?

Answer: P Since those are two examples of things that can have pretty far-reaching effects, I would say that if you only have a username and a password and you need to remove a wallet address or a team member, then yes, there will be a 24-hour hold before they are permanently removed.

But the wallet will instantly become not usable to have XMR sent to, or it won't be usable in a trade right away, and then within 24 hours, it will fully disappear. Because I can see the importance in why someone would take an action like that, and we can't force them to have to wait 24 hours because that could be very devastating for them.

And the same with removing a team member who could be malicious. That team member will instantly have their access removed; they just won't be deleted for 24 hours if that's the only security methods they have.

## G6
PGP login uses the key with no username. Is that fine?

Answer: Username to log in with is mandatory for every single account. They don't necessarily have to use it to get into the account, but it will be mandatory that they use it. They create one at sign up, a unique username and also a unique display name. Then they get to choose the other methods such as a password, a PGP key, a security key, or a passkey. But we will consider secure a username, PGP key, and display name. Those would be considered strong.

## G7
During someone's account hold, can their trade partner still open a dispute?

Answer: Just because a trade is on hold does not mean their account is on hold, so of course they can reach out to moderators and fill them in on new information. But even on trades that are on hold, they can still go into the trade and still message back and forth and add more context to the trade. It's just no currency is going to be moving back and forth until the moderator decides. So that trade they can still access, they can still message back and forth with the other person because all that context is going to help the moderator.

## G8

Answer: Yes, if an account is just on hold because they had a lack of security and tried to do a change which caused a 24-hour account hold. So basically just really means 24-hour time period for which they can't do any other major changes to the account. They can stay logged in and they could wait out the whole time if they wanted to and when the session runs out they can extend it if they want to. There's just not a lot they can do while they're in the account until the 24-hour hold is up. But of course when they initiate the hold it will strongly recommend that they increase their security to prevent these things from happening again.

## G16
If no date can be read on the screenshot, what happens?

If we can retrieve neither, because let's say they've already removed the EXIF data, and we cannot view the date in the photo with our automated screener, our proof screener, then a warning message will be displayed to the poster of the proof letting them know that we were unable to detect the date that this image was produced.

We are going to allow you to post it as proof, but a warning is being given to the seller with the same information. Please turn to the designer for wording there, but you know, that kind of thing. And then we'll warn the seller that we cannot independently confirm the date the image or proof was created, so please take some extra caution with this trade. But it also does not mean that it is not valid; we are just taking extra precaution by giving you this warning.
