## 4. Real logic conflict (stopped here): bans' site-ban gate vs main's FrozenWatch (D-016)

Playwright `e2e/banned.spec.ts:37` fails on the merge. It passes on the bans head (3/3).

A probe showed the cause: the app calls `GET /v1/account/freeze` (main's FrozenWatch) and the site-ban gate answers 403 `banned`. The client then sends the person back to `/account/banned`, so a site-banned trader cannot stay in their open trade's room.

The real API does the same: `siteBanAllows` (apps/api/src/banState.ts) does not list GET /v1/account/freeze.

There are two possible fixes, and each picks a behaviour, so this needs a decision:
- **(a)** Add `GET /v1/account/freeze` to the gate's ACCOUNT list, so a site-banned account with unfinished trades can read its own freezes.
- **(b)** Have FrozenWatch ignore a 403 `banned` instead of redirecting.

I recommend (a). It is an own-data read, and the room needs it to stay usable.

The same gate also does not list main's or this batch's other trade-room routes. The e2e did not hit them, but the lead should decide on them too:
- GET /v1/trades/:code/blur-checks and the two blur-check POSTs;
- the proof stack's GET/PUT /v1/trades/:code/proofs/:id (a buyer uploading proof to finish the trade);
- device-bound's POST /v1/account/session/proof(+challenge), when the flag is on;
- trades-list's GET /v1/trades.
