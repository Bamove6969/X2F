Decided this weekend

  • One ad lists many currencies and payment types; buyer picks both; cap 3 buy + 3 sell
  • Tier amounts converted into the buyer's currency
  • Buyer can dispute 30 minutes after "I've paid"; buyer can cancel (trade stays open until XMR is back)
  • Reviews show on completion; Skip shows nothing; optional "Completed over $X in trades"
  • One 16-character recovery code, required at signup
  • Password-only changes wait 24 h; recovery-code login logs everyone out
  • Session length picked at every login; vendors up to 24 h
  • Trader vs Vendor accounts; per-member permissions; ban list
