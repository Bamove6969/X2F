  1. Copy the encrypted message below.
  2. Open your PGP app (Kleopatra, GPG Suite or OpenKeychain) and decrypt it.
  3. Paste the code from inside the message.

The decrypted message also names X2F and today's date and time. If it names a different site or an old time, stop and don't paste anything. Someone may be trying to phish you.
Key verified

Every login works this way from now on. X2F shows a message locked to your key, your PGP app unlocks it with your private key, and you paste in the code.

Each code works once and expires after 5 minutes, so a fake site can't reuse one.
