B Plus Jakarta Sans + Inter + JetBrains Mono Recommended

Titles: Plus Jakarta Sans · Text: Inter · Numbers: JetBrains Mono

Soft, rounded geometric titles that stay smooth when big and bold; Inter is the most screen-tuned body face there is, crisp on Windows laptops. JetBrains Mono keeps 0/O and 1/l/I clearly apart for amounts and addresses.
